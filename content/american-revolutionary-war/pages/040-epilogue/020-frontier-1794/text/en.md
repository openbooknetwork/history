North of the Ohio, a confederacy of native nations defeats American armies in 1790 and 1791. On 20 August 1794 Anthony Wayne’s army defeats it at Fallen Timbers, and by the Treaty of Greenville of 3 August 1795 the nations cede most of present-day Ohio.

Jay’s Treaty with Britain, ratified in 1795, leads the British to hand over their forts on American soil in 1796, among them Detroit, Niagara and Oswego. Pinckney’s Treaty with Spain, signed on 27 October 1795, fixes the Florida border at the 31st parallel and opens the Mississippi to American trade.

Tennessee becomes the sixteenth state on 1 June 1796, the first made from a federal territory. In November 1800 the government moves to the new city of Washington.
