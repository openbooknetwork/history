The Allies invite both the Assembly and the [İstanbul](@istanbul) government to the peace conference. To leave Turkey with one government, the Assembly abolishes the sultanate on 1 November 1922 and keeps the caliphate for now. Mehmed VI leaves İstanbul on a British warship on 17 November. Six centuries of Ottoman rule end.
