Shortly after midnight on 29 May 1453 the assault begins along the whole land front and from the ships. The irregulars attack first, then İshak Pasha’s Anatolians, and before dawn Mehmed sends the janissaries against the stockade at the Mesoteichion. Karaca Pasha’s Rumelians attack the walls of Blachernae, Zaganos’s troops cross the bridge of barrels to the walls along the Horn, and Hamza Bey’s fleet attacks the sea walls.

[Giustiniani](@constantinople-assault-giustiniani) is badly wounded and is carried through the city to his ship in the harbour, and his Genoese follow him. Barbaro, a Venetian, writes that the wound is slight. The janissaries break through at the Fifth Military Gate north of the Lycus, and the defenders give way. Doukas tells that about 50 Ottomans get in by the [Kerkoporta](@kerkoporta), a small gate left open near the palace, and raise their flags on the wall.

What becomes of [Constantine XI](@constantinople-assault-constantine) is unknown. Most accounts have him killed fighting in the streets after he throws off his imperial insignia. Barbaro writes that he hangs himself, and some Ottoman accounts have him killed by Ottoman marines on his way to the Sea of Marmara.
