On the morning of the battle Bayezid draws up his army facing south. He stands in the [centre](@ankara-lines-bayezid) on a low rise with about 10,000 janissaries, the azaps in front of them, his sons İsa, Musa and Mustafa and the grand vizier Çandarlı Ali Pasha. [Stefan Lazarević](@ankara-lines-lazarevic) holds the right wing with his Serbian horsemen, his brother Vuk and Đurađ and Grgur Branković. [Süleyman Çelebi](@ankara-lines-suleyman) holds the left with the Anatolian troops, among them the men of Aydın, Saruhan, Menteşe and Germiyan, and [Mehmed Çelebi](@ankara-lines-mehmed) commands the reserve in the rear.

[Timur](@ankara-lines-timur) commands the centre, with the war elephants in front of it. His son Miran Shah leads the right wing and his son Shah Rukh the left, and his grandsons Muhammad Sultan, Abu Bakr, Pir Muhammad and Khalil Sultan lead the vanguard and divisions of the wings. Bayezid leaves troops on a hill behind his line, the hill Bıyıktay calls [Çataltepe](@ankara-cataltepe), against an encirclement.

Nizam ad-Din Shami and İsmail Hakkı Uzunçarşılı put the Rumelian troops with Süleyman on the left and the Anatolian troops on the right beside the Serbs. In later Turkish accounts Timur’s right under Abu Bakr opens the battle with arrows, and the Ottoman left drives it back.
