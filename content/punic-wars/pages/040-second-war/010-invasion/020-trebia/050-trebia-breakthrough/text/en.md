In the centre the Roman legions rout the 8,000 [Gauls](@trebia-breakthrough-gauls) and a unit of African infantry and break clean through Hannibal’s line. When they halt and re-form, the rest of their army behind them has dissolved. [Sempronius](@trebia-breakthrough-sempronius), who is fighting with the infantry, leads these 10,000 men away in formation, back across the Trebia to [Placentia](@placentia-218).

@image trebia-folard-66c8d2

The Carthaginians cut down the partly surrounded rest of the Roman army between the battle line and the river. Paul Erdkamp estimates 20,000 Romans killed, not counting prisoners. Hannibal loses several thousand of the Gauls in his centre, fewer of his other infantry and cavalry, and several elephants. Over the winter, by Polybius’s account, Gallic recruits bring his army to 60,000 men.
