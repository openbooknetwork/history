The Ilkhan Ghazan has Sultan Kayqubad III executed in 1302 and restores Mesud II in 1303. Mesud dies around 1308, the last Seljuk sultan of Rûm. Ilkhanid governors still command in central Anatolia, while the beys of the western marches rule their lands as independent princes and strike coins in their own names.

Germiyan at [Kütahya](@kutahya) is the strongest of the beyliks. Along the Aegean, from north to south, Karesi at [Balıkesir](@balikesir), Saruhan, Aydın and Menteşe at [Milas](@milas) take the Byzantine coastlands in these years. Inland lie Hamid at [Eğirdir](@egirdir) with its branch Teke at [Antalya](@antalya), Eşref at [Beyşehir](@beysehir) and Karaman at [Larende](@larende). Candar holds the hills of Paphlagonia and takes [Kastamonu](@kastamonu) from the Chobanids in 1309.

Osman’s land runs from Söğüt and Eskişehir to Bilecik, İnegöl and [Yenişehir](@yenisehir), at the edge of the plain of Bithynia. It is one of the smaller beyliks, and the one that borders the Byzantine cities of Bithynia. Ottoman tradition takes 1299 as the founding year of the state.
