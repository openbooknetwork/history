The Roman legions do not pursue the fleeing Iberians. They wheel outwards and attack the Africans and the Poeni from behind while the allied legions hold them in front. The Libyans resist hard and lose heavily before they break.

Hasdrubal escapes with most of his cavalry and elephants and a few infantry, and the Romans take his camp with its supplies and treasury. Ibera probably surrenders after the battle. The army that Carthage had raised under Mago to land in Italy is sent to Iberia instead, and the land route to Italy stays closed.
