After Manuel refuses his summons, Bayezid sets out to starve Constantinople into surrender. From 1394 Ottoman troops hold the land side of the city and cut it off from the countryside. Ships still reach it by sea, and the Genoese of Galata and the Venetians bring in grain, but bread grows scarce and dear, and many people leave the city.

To command the Bosporus, Bayezid builds a castle at [Güzelcehisar](@guzelcehisar) on the Asian shore, where the strait is narrowest, in 1394, or 1395 by other accounts. It is a tower about 25 metres high inside a wall.

John VII holds [Selymbria](@selymbria) on the Sea of Marmara, west of the capital. Pope Boniface IX proclaims a crusade against the Turks in 1394, and Manuel asks the rulers of the West for help.
