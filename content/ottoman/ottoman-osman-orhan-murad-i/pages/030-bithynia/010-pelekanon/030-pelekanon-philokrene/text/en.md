At dawn on 11 June the Byzantine army begins to withdraw along the coast toward [Philokrene](@philokrene). [Orhan’s horsemen](@pelekanon-philokrene-turks) come down from the hills and attack the columns. A rumour spreads that the emperor has been killed or mortally wounded, and the withdrawal turns into a rout with heavy Byzantine losses.

[Kantakouzenos](@pelekanon-philokrene-kantakouzenos) gathers the survivors at Philokrene and takes them back to Constantinople by sea. Andronikos III gives up the campaign, and no later Byzantine army tries to win back the lands in Asia. Nicaea and Nicomedia are left without relief.
