The Ottomans hold Šabac, which they call Böğürdelen, an important border fort on the lower Sava about 60 km west of [Belgrade](@belgrade-1476). In January 1474 Ali Bey Mihaloğlu, the bey of [Smederevo](@smederevo-1474), raids eastern Hungary, destroys [Oradea](@oradea-1474) (then Várad) and takes 16,000 prisoners. Matthias Corvinus is at war with the kings of Poland and Bohemia until late 1474 and then turns against the Ottomans.

@image sabac-fortress-e9607c

After the victory at Vaslui, Stephen of Moldavia swears loyalty to Matthias on 15 August 1475. Mehmed offers Matthias peace, but the king refuses and in the winter marches against Šabac. During the siege Matthias is nearly captured while he watches the fort from a boat, and the fort falls to him on 15 February 1476.

@image black-army-soldier-42156b

At the same time Matthias sends Vlad III, whom he has released from his prison in 1475, and the Serbian Vuk Grgurević into Bosnia. In February and March 1476 they take [Srebrenica](@srebrenica-1476) and other forts and lay waste to Srebrenica, Kušlat and [Zvornik](@zvornik-1476), and Vlad has the Ottoman prisoners impaled.
