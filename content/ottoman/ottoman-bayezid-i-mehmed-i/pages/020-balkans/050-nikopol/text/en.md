On his way back Bayezid takes [Nikopol](@nikopol-1393) from the north and has Ivan Shishman put to death on 3 June 1395, by the Anonymous Bulgarian Chronicle. A Byzantine chronicle gives 29 October, and some accounts have Shishman die in prison. His lands are annexed, and of the Bulgarian rulers only Ivan Sratsimir of Vidin remains, as Bayezid’s vassal.

The lands of Marko and Konstantin Dragaš pass under direct Ottoman rule and form a province around Konstantin’s seat at [Velbužd](@kyustendil-1395), which the Ottomans name Köstendil after him. Marko’s town of [Prilep](@prilep-1395) becomes part of it.

In July 1395 Sigismund leads an army from [Brașov](@brasov) into Wallachia with Mircea to restore him. He takes Little Nicopolis (Turnu), across the Danube from Nikopol, and leaves a Hungarian garrison there. In the Morea, Evrenos joins the Navarrese of Achaea in a raid against Theodore and takes the castle of [Akova](@akova-1395).
