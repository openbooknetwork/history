The battle opens at 6 am, when a French grand battery of 102 guns fires on the Russian centre. Davout sends Jean Dominique Compans’s division against the southernmost flèche, with Joseph Dessaix’s division echeloned to its left. Both generals are wounded and Davout is thrown when his horse is shot under him, but by 7:30 the French hold the three flèches.

Bagration counterattacks and throws them out, Ney retakes the earthworks, and Barclay sends guard regiments, grenadiers and guns from the Russian right to help the 2nd Army. The French make seven assaults on the flèches. At about 11 am Bagration is hit in the leg by splinters of a cannonball, and by 11:30 Louis Friant’s division holds the position and the Russians fall back behind the village of Semyonovskaya. Bagration dies of the wound on 24 September.

On the French left Eugène’s corps takes the village of Borodino from the Russian Guard jägers. In the south Poniatowski’s Poles take Utitsa and its mound on the Old Smolensk road, Nikolay Tuchkov is mortally wounded while retaking them by 8 am, and the Poles and Junot’s Westphalians take the village again.
