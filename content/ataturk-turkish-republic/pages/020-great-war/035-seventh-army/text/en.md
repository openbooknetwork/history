In July 1917 Mustafa Kemal is given the Seventh Army, part of the Yıldırım Army Group that the German general Erich von Falkenhayn is assembling at Aleppo. He does not get on with Falkenhayn. With İsmet Bey he reports to the Grand Vizier, Talat Pasha, that the army lacks men and supplies, and proposes a defensive line in Syria under Turkish command. When the proposal is rejected he resigns, and in October he returns to İstanbul.

In December he travels by train with the heir to the throne, Vahdettin, on a state visit to Germany. He visits the Western Front and tells Kaiser Wilhelm II and his generals that the Central Powers will lose the war. In the summer of 1918 he is treated for illness in Vienna and Karlsbad. While he is there, Mehmed V dies and Vahdettin becomes Sultan Mehmed VI on 4 July 1918.
