In late September 1920 Kâzım Karabekir's Eastern Front attacks the Armenian republic. It takes Sarıkamış, then Kars on 30 October and Gümrü in November. The campaign frees the eastern divisions for the war in the west.

The Treaty of Gümrü is signed on 3 December 1920. Armenia gives up Kars and renounces the Treaty of Sèvres. The Armenian government has handed power to the Soviets the day before, and the treaty is never ratified. The Treaty of Kars fixes the border in October 1921.
