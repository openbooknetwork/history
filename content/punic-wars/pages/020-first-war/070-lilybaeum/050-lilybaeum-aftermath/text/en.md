Shortly after the battle Carthalo joins Adherbal with 70 ships. Adherbal brings Carthalo’s squadron up to 100 and sends Carthalo to [Lilybaeum](@lilybaeum-249), where he burns several Roman ships. Later in the year Carthalo meets a Roman supply convoy off [Phintias](@phintias-249), 800 transports escorted by 120 warships, and harries it until a storm sinks every Roman ship but two.

Pulcher is recalled to Rome and tried for treason. He is convicted of the lesser charge of sacrilege over the chickens, narrowly escapes a death sentence and goes into exile. Unchallenged at sea, Carthage lays up most of its fleet, and Rome’s next large fleet sails only in 242 BC.
