Mehmed now has a road built from the Bosporus over the hill behind [Galata](@galata-1453) to the Golden Horn. His men clear the trees and lay timbers greased with oil along the track. On the night of 21 to 22 April 1453, oxen and men draw 72 ships on rollers from the shore at [Dolmabahçe](@constantinople-overland-dolmabahce) up the valley and down into the Horn at [Kasımpaşa](@constantinople-overland-kasimpasa), a distance of 2 to 4 km. Guns on the hills by Galata fire on the Christian ships in the Horn while the ships are hauled.

@image zonaro-ships-overland-6e6431

With Ottoman ships inside the chain, the defenders must man the walls along the Horn as well. On the night of 28 April the Venetian [Giacomo Coco](@constantinople-overland-coco) leads ships and fire ships against the Ottoman ships at Kasımpaşa. The Genoese of Galata have had the attack put off by a day and have warned the Ottomans, whose guns sink Coco’s galley, and the attack fails.

@image golden-horn-galata-1880-3439c5

Mehmed has the sailors who swim ashore, about 40, impaled in sight of the walls, and the defenders kill their 260 Ottoman prisoners on the walls in reply. The guns on the hills by Galata go on firing on the ships and the Horn walls until 14 May, when Mehmed moves them to Blachernae.
