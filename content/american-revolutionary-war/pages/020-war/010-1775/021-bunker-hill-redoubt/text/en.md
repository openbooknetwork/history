On the night of 16 June Colonel William [Prescott](@bunker-hill-redoubt-prescott) leads about 1,200 men onto the Charlestown peninsula. Instead of Bunker Hill, the higher ground near the Neck, they fortify Breed’s Hill, closer to Boston, with a square earthen redoubt about 40 metres on a side. At dawn HMS Lively opens fire, and soon the ships in the harbour and the battery on [Copp’s Hill](@bunker-hill-redoubt-copps-hill) join in, with little effect.

Daylight shows that the redoubt can be outflanked, and Prescott’s men dig a breastwork running down the hill. Captain Thomas Knowlton’s Connecticut men hold a rail fence packed with hay further back towards the Mystic River, and about 200 New Hampshire men under John Stark and James Reed extend it with a stone wall down the beach to the water. General Israel [Putnam](@bunker-hill-redoubt-putnam) tries to send forward the men gathering on Bunker Hill.

General William [Howe](@bunker-hill-redoubt-howe) lands about 1,500 men at Moulton’s Point in the early afternoon. Seeing the defenders on Bunker Hill, he asks Boston for more troops, and Brigadier General Robert [Pigot](@bunker-hill-redoubt-pigot) assembles a second force south of Charlestown village.
