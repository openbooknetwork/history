Süleyman Şah of Germiyan, wary of both Karaman and the Ottomans, marries his daughter Devletşah Hatun to Murad’s son Bayezid. The wedding is dated 1378 or 1381. Her dowry is the Germiyan capital [Kütahya](@kutahya-1381) with [Simav](@simav-1381), Eğrigöz (Emet) and [Tavşanlı](@tavsanli-1381), and the 2,000 to 3,000 Ottoman troops who escort the wedding party take over smaller places as well. Bayezid becomes governor at Kütahya, and Süleyman Şah moves his seat to [Kula](@kula-1381). Devletşah is often confused with Devlet Hatun, another consort of Bayezid and the mother of Mehmed I.

Murad also buys from Hüseyin Bey of Hamid the eastern towns of his beylik, among them [Akşehir](@aksehir-1381) and [Beyşehir](@beysehir-1381). The Ottoman chronicles add Yalvaç and Seydişehir and give the price as 80,000 gold pieces. The Hamid beys keep [Eğirdir](@egirdir) and Isparta.

Ottoman land now runs from the Sea of Marmara to the edge of the [Konya](@konya-karaman) plain and borders Karaman, whose bey Alaeddin Ali has been married to Murad’s daughter Nefise since 1378.
