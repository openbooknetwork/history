On 16 July the Greeks make a feint towards the Turkish right at Eskişehir while their main blow falls on the left. They then wheel north and roll up the Turkish defence with frontal attacks combined with flanking moves. İsmet counterattacks hard to save Eskişehir, but the city falls.

Mustafa Kemal judges that the army matters more than the ground. On his advice İsmet breaks contact and takes the Western Front back to the east bank of the Sakarya, about 80 km from Ankara, with heavy losses but without being encircled. The Greek leadership meets at Kütahya and, after a delay of almost a month, decides to march on Ankara.
