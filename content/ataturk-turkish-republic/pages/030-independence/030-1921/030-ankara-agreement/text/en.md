After Sakarya, France settles with Ankara. The agreement of 20 October 1921, signed by Henry Franklin-Bouillon and Yusuf Kemal Bey, ends the fighting in Cilicia and fixes the border with French Syria. Alexandretta stays with Syria under a special regime. French troops leave Adana, Antep and the rest of Cilicia in December 1921 and January 1922. They had already given up Maraş and Urfa in 1920.

The Treaty of Kars of 13 October 1921 with the Soviet republics of the Caucasus settles the eastern border. Only the Greek front remains.
