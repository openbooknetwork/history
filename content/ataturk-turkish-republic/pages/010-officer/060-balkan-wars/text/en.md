In the First Balkan War, from October 1912, the Balkan states take almost all of Ottoman Europe. [Thessaloniki](@salonica-1912) (then Salonica) falls to Greece in November.

Mustafa Kemal is head of operations of the [Bulair](@bolayir) Corps, which guards the neck of the Gallipoli peninsula and the land approach to the Dardanelles. The corps' attack at Bulair on 8 February 1913 fails. [Edirne](@edirne) surrenders to Bulgarian and Serbian forces in March. Ottoman forces retake it in July 1913, during the Second Balkan War.
