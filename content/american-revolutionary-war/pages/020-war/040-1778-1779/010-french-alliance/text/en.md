On 6 February 1778 France signs a treaty of alliance with the United States, and on 17 March Britain declares war on France. Henry Clinton replaces Howe and evacuates Philadelphia on 18 June. He marches across New Jersey towards Sandy Hook, where the navy will carry his army to New York.

Washington catches the rear guard at Monmouth Court House on 28 June, in heat above 32 °C. The battle ends in a draw, and Clinton reaches New York.

A French fleet under d’Estaing arrives off Rhode Island at the end of July. In August the allies besiege Newport, held by the British since December 1776. A storm scatters the fleets, d’Estaing withdraws to Boston for repairs, and the American troops pull back after the Battle of Rhode Island on 29 August. The British leave Newport in October 1779 and take the garrison to New York.
