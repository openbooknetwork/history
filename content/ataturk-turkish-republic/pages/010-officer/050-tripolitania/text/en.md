When Italy invades Ottoman Libya in 1911, the empire cannot send an army by sea. Young officers volunteer and travel there in disguise, through Egypt. Mustafa Kemal reaches Tobruk in December 1911 and commands at [Derna](@derna) from March 1912, organising local Arab fighters against the Italian beachheads. The Balkan War pulls him home in October 1912.
