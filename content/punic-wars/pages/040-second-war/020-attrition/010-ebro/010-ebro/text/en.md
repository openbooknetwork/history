In autumn 218 BC, while Hannibal marches on Italy, Gnaeus Cornelius Scipio sails from Massalia and lands at Emporion with two Roman and two allied legions, about 25,000 men. Hanno, the Carthaginian commander north of the Ebro, attacks him at Cissa without waiting for Hasdrubal and is defeated. Tarraco becomes the Roman base.

In spring 217 BC Hasdrubal Barca advances north by land and sea together. At the mouth of the Ebro 55 Roman and Massaliot ships defeat his fleet of 40 quinqueremes and take or sink 29 of them. Later that year Publius Scipio joins his brother with 8,000 men.

Carthage orders Hasdrubal to take his army to Italy. In spring 215 BC he marches north from New Carthage, while the Scipios cross the Ebro and besiege Ibera, a town allied to Carthage on its south bank. When Hasdrubal besieges a town allied to Rome, the brothers lift their siege and march to meet him.
