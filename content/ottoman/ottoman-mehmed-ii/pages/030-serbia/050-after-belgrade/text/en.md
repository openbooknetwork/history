Plague breaks out in the crusader camp. Hunyadi dies of it at [Zemun](@zemun-1456) on 11 August 1456, and Capistrano dies at [Ilok](@ilok-1456) on 23 October. Hunyadi’s elder son Ladislaus becomes captain general of Hungary. His quarrel with Count Ulrich of Celje, a kinsman of King Ladislaus V, ends when his men kill Ulrich at [Belgrade](@belgrade-ulrich-1456) on 9 November 1456. Mehmed blames Zaganos Pasha for the defeat and sends him away to Balıkesir. Mahmud Pasha, who has fought well at Belgrade, becomes grand vizier.

Despot Đurađ Branković dies on 24 December 1456. His son Lazar, at [Smederevo](@smederevo-1456), makes a treaty with Mehmed on 15 January 1457 that gives him back most of his father’s lands. In Hungary the king has Ladislaus Hunyadi beheaded at Buda in March 1457 and takes his younger brother Matthias to Prague. King Ladislaus dies there on 23 November 1457. Michael Szilágyi comes to the diet with 15,000 men, and on 24 January 1458 the nobles gathered on the frozen Danube at [Pest](@budapest-1458) proclaim the 14-year-old Matthias king.

Before the siege Hunyadi backed Vlad, a son of Vlad Dracul, against Vladislav II of Wallachia. Vlad invades Wallachia with Hungarian help in 1456, in April, July or August by different accounts, and Vladislav dies in the fighting. As Vlad III he rules from [Târgoviște](@targoviste-1456) and sends the sultan his tribute. In spring 1457 his cousin Stephen marches into Moldavia with 6,000 men and Vlad’s help, drives out Peter Aaron and becomes voivode on 14 April 1457. He rules from [Suceava](@suceava-1457).
