# Research for Joan of Arc

## 070 after-her

Coverage: primary (the Journal d'un bourgeois de Paris for 1431 and 1436; Monstrelet's Chronicles in Johnes's translation, with the continuation of book 3 for Formigny and Castillon; Murray's translation of the rehabilitation with his introductory note; Quicherat 5 for the Orléans chronicle of the feast and the city accounts; the bull Divina disponente and the homily of 16 May 1920 in AAS 12) and scholarly (Ramsay's Lancaster and York, vols. 1-2; Oman's Art of War, vol. 2; Beaucourt's Histoire de Charles VII, vols. 2 and 5; Lowell; Thurston; Renoux; France's Life, read only for where to look), with Wikipedia for the order of events. Ramsay 1 and 2 are one work, Beaucourt 2 and 5 one work, and Monstrelet A and B one chronicle whose book 3 is its continuation: no author is used with two distinct works, so no later work withdraws an earlier one. Unread: the two modern studies of the Orléans feast and W. P. Müller on the rehabilitation (bot walls, see sources.md "Unread"), and Pernoud's Retrial (lent only). The Truce of Tours is read only in Ramsay 2 and Wikipedia, and the 1793 suspension and 1803 renewal of the Orléans feast only in France's Life and Wikipedia.
