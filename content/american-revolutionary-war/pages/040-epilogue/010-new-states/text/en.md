Vermont, an independent republic since 1777 and claimed by New York, pays $30,000 to settle the New York land claims and is admitted as the fourteenth state on 4 March 1791. Ten of the twelve amendments sent to the states in 1789 are ratified by 15 December 1791 and become the Bill of Rights.

In 1791 Pierre Charles L’Enfant lays out the plan of the federal city in the district on the Potomac. Kentucky, which held a series of conventions at Danville to separate from Virginia, becomes the fifteenth state on 1 June 1792.
