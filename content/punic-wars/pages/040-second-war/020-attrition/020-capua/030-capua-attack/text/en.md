In 211 BC Hannibal marches back from the south and camps close to the Roman lines. He attacks the outer line while the Carthaginian garrison of Capua under Bostar and Hanno, with the Capuans, makes a sortie against the inner one.

An Iberian cohort with three elephants breaks through the outer line and reaches the rampart of a Roman camp, but is destroyed before it can break in. Livy writes that Fulvius Flaccus holds off Hannibal while Appius Claudius drives the Capuans back to their gates and is wounded there. The attack on the lines fails.
