Mehmed II of Karaman was killed early in 1423 while besieging the Ottoman port of [Antalya](@antalya-1423), and his son Ibrahim fights his uncle Ali for the throne. Ibrahim wins it with Ottoman help in 1424 and gives the Hamid lands around [Isparta](@isparta) back to Murad. In 1425 he marries Murad’s sister İlaldı Hatun, a daughter of Mehmed I, and in 1427 his brothers İsa and Alaeddin Ali marry two more daughters of Mehmed I. In 1428, while Murad is at Golubac, Ibrahim takes the Hamid lands again.

Yakub II of Germiyan, who had backed Küçük Mustafa, makes his peace with Murad. Yakub has no sons and leaves his land to Murad by will. In 1428, an old man, he travels from [Kütahya](@kutahya) to [Bursa](@bursa) and visits the graves of Osman and Orhan, and Murad receives him at [Edirne](@edirne) with a lavish ceremony, where Yakub declares his will. Yakub dies in January 1429 and is buried in the complex that he had built at Kütahya, and Murad annexes Germiyan.

Plague breaks out in the Ottoman lands in 1428 and 1429. It kills the sheikh Emir Sultan at Bursa, the vizier and builder Hacı İvaz Pasha, the grand vizier Çandarlı İbrahim Pasha and Murad’s younger brothers Mahmud and Yusuf, whom Murad had blinded. With Germiyan, the Ottoman lands in Anatolia reach from the Aegean to Kütahya, Ankara and Amasya and south to Antalya. Karaman holds the centre and the south coast east of Antalya, and Candar the north coast from Kastamonu to Sinop.
