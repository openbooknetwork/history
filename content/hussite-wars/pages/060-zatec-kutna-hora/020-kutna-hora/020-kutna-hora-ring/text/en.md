On 21 December 1421, the feast of St Thomas, the Hussites and the men of [Kutná Hora](@kutna-hora-ring-town) march out of the Kouřim gate after a short breakfast and stop about 400 m from the walls. There they close themselves in with their wagons, with many guns on the wagons, by Laurence of Březová. The royal army comes on in several bodies. In the largest, many hundreds of oxen and cows are driven among the armed men, to frighten the Hussites and cover the horse against the guns.

@image kutna-hora-panorama-2d3187

The wagons stand chained in a ring. Each time a royal body charges them, the guns on the wagons drive it back with heavy losses, and the fighting goes on until night. Meanwhile exiles from Kutná Hora let many hundreds of royal troops into the town through the Kolín gate, and the Hussites in the town are killed, except those who know an agreed sign. The wagon fort is cut off from the town and its food.
