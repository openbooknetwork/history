In 237 BC Hamilcar Barca takes the army of the Mercenary War to Iberia and probably lands at Gades. Carthage at this time holds only a few towns on the southern coast. Before he leaves, by the account Polybius gives, Hamilcar makes his nine-year-old son Hannibal swear never to be a friend of Rome.

In eight years of war and diplomacy Hamilcar wins the south and east of the peninsula, with the silver mines of the Sierra Morena, and founds Akra Leuke. He dies fighting an Iberian tribe in 228 BC, and his son-in-law Hasdrubal the Fair takes command. Hasdrubal extends Carthaginian rule mostly by diplomacy and founds Qart Hadasht, New Carthage, as its capital and naval base. In 226 BC a treaty with Rome makes the river Ebro the northern limit of Carthaginian expansion.

In 221 BC Hasdrubal is killed by a slave avenging his Celtic master. The army chooses Hannibal, then 26 years old, as its commander, and Carthage confirms him. In 221 and 220 BC Hannibal campaigns on the Meseta against the Olcades and the Vaccaei, and on the way back defeats a coalition led by the Carpetani at the Tagus. At some date after 226 BC Rome takes the town of Saguntum, south of the Ebro, under its protection.
