In January 1921 the Greek army advances from Bursa towards Eskişehir. İsmet's Western Front stops it at İnönü between 6 and 11 January, the first victory of the new regular army. In February and March the Ankara government takes part in the London Conference, and on 16 March it signs a treaty with Soviet Russia in Moscow.

A second Greek offensive, from Bursa and from Uşak, begins on 23 March. İsmet stops the northern advance at İnönü again by 1 April. The southern column takes Afyon, and Refet Pasha's troops retake it in April.
