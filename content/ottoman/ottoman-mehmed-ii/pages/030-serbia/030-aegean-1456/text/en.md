In 1455 Mehmed takes [Foça](@foca-1455) (Phocaea) on the Aegean coast from the Genoese. The Genoese have held the town and its rich alum mines since the 13th century. Alum fixes dyes in cloth, and it is one of the most valuable goods that Genoese merchants trade.

The Genoese family of the Gattilusi, lords of Lesbos, hold the port of [Enez](@enez-1456) (Ainos) on the coast of Thrace and the islands of [Gökçeada](@imbros-1456) (Imbros), [Samothrace](@samothrace-1456), [Lemnos](@lemnos-1456) and [Thasos](@thasos-1456). After 1453 they keep them for a short time as vassals of the sultan. In 1456 the Ottomans take Enez and the islands from them. Lesbos stays with the Gattilusi of [Mytilene](@mytilene-1456), who pay 4,000 gold pieces a year for it.

Michael Kritovoulos, a landowner and local leader on Gökçeada, helps to hand over Gökçeada, Lemnos and Thasos to the Ottomans without fighting, and Mehmed makes him governor of Gökçeada. Kritovoulos later writes a history of Mehmed’s reign in Greek. Some modern lists put the taking of Gökçeada, Lemnos and Thasos in 1453.
