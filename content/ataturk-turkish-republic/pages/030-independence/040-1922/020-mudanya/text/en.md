In September Turkish troops enter the neutral zone of the Straits and face British troops at Çanakkale. France and Italy refuse to support military action, and Britain agrees to talks.

The armistice signed at Mudanya on 11 October 1922 requires the Greek army to leave Eastern Thrace up to the Maritsa river within fifteen days. Eastern Thrace, Edirne included, returns to Turkish administration without a fight. Greece accepts the armistice a few days later, which ends the war.
