For eleven months after Sakarya the two armies face each other on a line from the Sea of Marmara past Eskişehir and Afyon. Ankara buys arms from Soviet Russia, Italy and France, moves divisions from the eastern and southern fronts, and trains. The plan, kept from almost everyone, is to strike the Greek southern wing at Afyon from the south and cut it off from İzmir and from the Greek corps at Eskişehir. In August Mustafa Kemal leaves Ankara in secret, letting it be known that he is hosting a tea party, and goes to the front headquarters at Akşehir.
