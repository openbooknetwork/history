On 26 October 1452 the papal legate Cardinal Isidore arrives in [Constantinople](@constantinople) with Leonard of Chios, the Latin archbishop of Mytilene, and 200 archers. On 12 December 1452 Isidore celebrates a Catholic liturgy in [Hagia Sophia](@hagia-sophia-1452), with Constantine XI and his court present, and proclaims the union of the churches agreed at Florence in 1439. Many of the city’s Greeks oppose the union.

In January 1453 the Genoese captain Giovanni Giustiniani Longo arrives with two ships and 700 men, 400 of them from Genoa and 300 from [Chios](@chios-1453). Constantine gives him command of the land walls and promises him the island of Lemnos. The captains of the Venetian ships in the harbour agree to stay and fight, but in February seven Venetian and Cretan ships slip away with about 700 men.

Karaca Pasha, Mehmed’s beylerbey of Rumelia, marches with 10,000 men against the Byzantine towns outside the city. [Mesembria](@mesembria-1453) and [Anchialos](@anchialos-1453) on the Black Sea surrender to him, and [Selymbria](@selymbria-1453) on the Sea of Marmara holds out. His men repair the roads and bridges from [Edirne](@edirne-1453) for the guns, and Orban’s great gun is drawn by 60 oxen. Baltaoğlu Süleyman Bey gathers the fleet at [Gallipoli](@gallipoli-1453), and on 23 March 1453 Mehmed leads the army out of Edirne.
