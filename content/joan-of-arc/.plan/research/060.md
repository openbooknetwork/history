## 060 rouen

Coverage: primary and scholarly. The trial of condemnation is read in Barrett's translation of the record (with Champion's sketches of Cauchon, Le Maistre and John of Luxembourg in the same file) and in Murray's, with Murray's depositions of 1450-1456 for the witnesses of the prison, Saint-Ouen and the Old Market (Toutmouille, Isambart de La Pierre, Ladvenu, Massieu, Taquel, Leparmentier, Guesdon). Quicherat 5 gives the documents of the price and the pay: the Norman levy of September 1430, a receipt of 6 December 1430 for the king's ten thousand livres, and the king's taxation of twenty sous a day to the Paris masters. Monstrelet A copies Henry VI's letter to the Duke of Burgundy on the sentence, and the Bourgeois tells the burning as it was reported in Paris. Ramsay 1 ch. 29, Lowell ch. 17-24, Lang ch. 21-26, France vol. 2 ch. 8-14 and Thurston follow the record and the documents with notes. The Wikipedia articles of the section were opened for the order of events and no line rests on them. The coast road from Le Crotoy by Saint-Valery, Eu and Dieppe is told only by Père Ignace's history of 1657, which Quicherat 5 prints with a warning and France follows (060.24). No modern study of the trial was read (the Caen and Rennes volumes are behind a bot wall, see sources.md "Unread").
