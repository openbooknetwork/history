On 22 December 1789 North Carolina’s legislature votes to cede the state’s land west of the mountains to the federal government. Congress accepts the cession on 2 April 1790 and on 26 May organises it as the Territory South of the River Ohio, usually called the Southwest Territory. William Blount becomes its governor, with his seat at Rocky Mount.

The Residence Act of 16 July 1790 places the permanent capital on the Potomac, in a district of up to ten miles square to be chosen by the president between the Eastern Branch and the Conococheague. Until it is ready, Congress sits in Philadelphia from December 1790.
