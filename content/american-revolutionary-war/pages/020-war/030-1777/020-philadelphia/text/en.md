Howe embarks about 17,000 troops at New York, sails round into the Chesapeake and lands at Head of Elk, about fifty miles southwest of Philadelphia, in late August. Washington meets him on the Brandywine on 11 September and is outflanked again. Congress moves to Lancaster and then to York. The British enter Philadelphia on 26 September, and Washington’s attack at Germantown on 4 October fails in the fog.

At York, on 15 November, Congress completes the Articles of Confederation and sends them to the states for ratification. Washington takes about 12,000 men into winter camp at Valley Forge, eighteen miles from the city. Between 1,700 and 2,000 of them die of disease. In June 1778 the army marches out drilled by Baron von Steuben.
