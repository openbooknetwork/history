Through the winter [Scipio](@great-plains-camps-scipio) negotiates with Syphax, who offers to arrange a peace. With each embassy he sends officers dressed as slaves, who report that the [Carthaginian camp](@great-plains-camps-carthaginian) has timber huts behind earth ramparts, while the [Numidian camp](@great-plains-camps-numidian) has huts of reed and thatch and no clear perimeter. In spring he prepares openly to assault [Utica](@utica-204), then marches out late one evening and divides his army.

[Laelius and Masinissa](@great-plains-camps-laelius) attack the Numidian camp and set the reed huts on fire. Many Carthaginians, taking the blaze for an accident, run unarmed from their own camp to help, and Scipio storms their camp and fires its huts. In the dark and the panic both armies lose heavily. Hasdrubal escapes 40 km to Carthage with 2,500 men, and Syphax with a few horsemen.
