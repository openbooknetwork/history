The Romans then turn on the Carthaginians north of the city. The light infantry skirmish and soon fall back, the infantry meet in the centre and the cavalry fight on the flanks. The Romans gain the upper hand and the Carthaginians retreat to the towns nearby. The sources leave it unclear why both besieging armies give up so quickly.

The Romans march south and besiege Syracuse, but without enough troops or secure supplies they soon withdraw. In 263 BC both consuls cross to Sicily with 40,000 men. Hiero makes peace, pays 100 talents of silver and becomes an ally of Rome, supplying the Roman army on the island.
