Back on the Palestine front as commander of the Seventh Army in August 1918, he faces Allenby's offensive at Megiddo on 19 September. The Ottoman line collapses. He brings what is left of his army north through Damascus to Aleppo and, on 26 October, holds a line at Katma north of the city, on roughly the border Türkiye has today.
