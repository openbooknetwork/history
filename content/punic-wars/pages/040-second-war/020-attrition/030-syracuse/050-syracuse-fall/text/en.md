Cut off from supplies, the inner city starves, and Syracusan envoys open talks with Marcellus. An Iberian captain named Moeriscus, one of the three commanders of Achradina, agrees to let the Romans in near the fountain of Arethusa on Ortygia. On the agreed signal, while the Romans make a diversionary attack on Achradina, he opens the gate. Ortygia falls, and Achradina surrenders.

Marcellus sets guards on the houses of the pro-Roman faction and gives the city to his soldiers to plunder. Archimedes, about 78 years old, is working at home when a Roman soldier who does not know him kills him, against Marcellus’s orders. By 210 BC the Romans have destroyed the last Carthaginian forces in Sicily and hold the whole island as a province.
