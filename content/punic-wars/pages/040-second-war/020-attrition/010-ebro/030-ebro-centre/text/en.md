The skirmishers exchange missiles and fall back, and the two [Roman legions](@ebro-centre-legions) advance on the [Iberian infantry](@ebro-centre-iberians). After an exchange of javelins the Iberians break and flee almost without fighting. Livy writes that they knew a victory would mean following Hasdrubal to Italy, and did not want to leave Iberia.

The legions push into the gap. On the wings the cavalry skirmish without result. The [Africans](@ebro-centre-africans) and the [Poeni](@ebro-centre-poeni) partly turn to strike the flanks of the Roman legions, but each also has an allied legion in front of it.
