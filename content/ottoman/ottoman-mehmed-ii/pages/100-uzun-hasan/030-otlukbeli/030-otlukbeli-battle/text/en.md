After the defeat on the river Uzun Hasan's army disappears, and Mehmed marches towards Bayburt for six days without news of it. On Wednesday 11 August 1473 the Ottomans halt at Üç Ağız, a narrow place near Tercan where three valleys meet, to rest their tired animals. At noon, while the army is still out of order, Gavur İshak's men appear on the hill of Otlukbeli. Davud Pasha, the beylerbey of Anatolia, attacks them with the Anatolian troops and throws them back to Uzun Hasan's main army, which holds the ridge above.

The Ottomans form their line with [Şehzade Mustafa](@otlukbeli-battle-mustafa) on the left, [Şehzade Bayezid](@otlukbeli-battle-bayezid) on the right and the janissaries with the guns and the light mortars in the centre. On the left Davud Pasha's men stand on the plain against the Aq Qoyunlu right under Uzun Hasan's son [Zeynel](@otlukbeli-battle-zeynel), and Mustafa falls on Zeynel's wing with his troops. Zeynel is killed among the azebs, the Ottoman foot soldiers, by one Mahmud Ağa, and his wing breaks. On the right Bayezid attacks [Uğurlu Mehmed](@otlukbeli-battle-ughurlu), who holds a stream against him and then withdraws, and Bayezid fights on against the men of Mehmed Bakır.

When the Aq Qoyunlu right has broken, [Mehmed](@otlukbeli-battle-mehmed) sends forward his 25,000 janissaries. [Uzun Hasan](@otlukbeli-battle-uzun) leaves the field and leaves Pir Mehmed Bey, who looks like him, in his place, and the Ottomans take Pir Mehmed and bring Uzun Hasan's standard to the sultan. The Aq Qoyunlu army breaks and flees, and many of its men are killed. Mehmed does not pursue it, because the ground is good for ambushes.
