On 3 July 1908 Ahmed Niyazi, a Unionist officer, leaves the garrison at Resne for the hills with a band of volunteers, and other officers follow across Macedonia. On the night of 23 to 24 July Abdülhamid II restores the constitution of 1876 and recalls parliament. Mustafa Kemal, a staff officer of the Third Army in Selanik, plays a small part in the revolution. At the end of the year he is sent to put down a tribal revolt in Tripolitania.

On 13 April 1909, the 31st of March in the calendar then in use, troops in İstanbul mutiny against the Committee of Union and Progress and call for the restoration of sharia law. Units of the Third Army form the Action Army and travel by rail to Yeşilköy, just west of the capital. Mustafa Kemal goes with them as a staff officer. The Action Army takes İstanbul on 24 April, and three days later parliament deposes Abdülhamid II and sends him into exile in Selanik.
