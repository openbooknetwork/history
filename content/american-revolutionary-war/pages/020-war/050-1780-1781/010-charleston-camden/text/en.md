Clinton besieges Charleston by land and sea and takes it on 12 May 1780 with Benjamin Lincoln’s whole army, more than five thousand men. It is the largest American surrender of the war. Clinton returns to New York and leaves Cornwallis to hold the Carolinas.

Horatio Gates, the victor of Saratoga, takes command of the southern army in July. At Camden on 16 August the army is routed when the militia break before a British bayonet charge. Gates reaches Charlotte, sixty miles away, the same evening.

The Carolina back country becomes a civil war of Loyalist and Patriot militia. On 7 October frontier riflemen surround Patrick Ferguson’s Loyalist corps on Kings Mountain and destroy it. Cornwallis withdraws from Charlotte back into South Carolina. In the same weeks Benedict Arnold’s plot to hand West Point to the British is discovered, and he escapes to New York.
