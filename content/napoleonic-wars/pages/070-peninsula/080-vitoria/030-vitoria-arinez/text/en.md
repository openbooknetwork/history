At noon Graham’s column appears on the Bilbao road, and Jourdan, seeing the danger of being surrounded, orders Gazan to fall back towards Vitoria. Graham drives Sarrut’s division back across the river but cannot force the crossing against Reille’s troops. Further east Longa’s Spaniards defeat Joseph’s Spanish Royal Guard and cut the road to Bayonne.

Picton’s 3rd Division of Dalhousie’s column crosses the river with Kempt’s help. The French guns, 40 to 50 of them by Picton’s account, and a counterattack on its open flank cost it 1,800 men, more than a third of all allied losses in the battle. Cole’s 4th Division crosses further west, and when Gazan and d’Erlon try to stand at the village of Arinez, the 4th, Light, 3rd and 7th Divisions take it in one line.
