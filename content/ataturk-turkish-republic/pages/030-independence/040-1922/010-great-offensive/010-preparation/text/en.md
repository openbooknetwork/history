For eleven months after Sakarya the two armies face each other on a line from the Sea of Marmara past Eskişehir and Afyon. Ankara buys arms from Soviet Russia, Italy and France, moves divisions from the eastern and southern fronts, and trains. The plan, kept from almost everyone, is to strike the Greek southern wing at Afyon from the south and cut the whole army off from İzmir. In August Mustafa Kemal slips out of Ankara, letting it be known he is hosting a tea party, and reaches the front headquarters at Akşehir.
