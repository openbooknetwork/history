After the defeat on the river Mehmed has his guns fire on the walls day and night. The defenders fire back, and a cannon ball kills [Karaca Pasha](@belgrade-assault-karaca), the beylerbey of Rumelia. The Ottomans attack in small numbers every day and fill the ditch before the walls with earth, and the guns open several breaches in the walls. Before the assault [Hunyadi](@belgrade-assault-hunyadi) brings about 4,000 more men into the fortress.

On 21 July at sundown Mehmed orders a general assault, which goes on through the night. His troops flood into the town and attack the upper town, and the janissaries break through its weaker eastern walls. Hunyadi has the defenders throw tarred wood and brushwood into the breaches and set it alight. A [wall of fire](@belgrade-assault-fire) cuts off the janissaries inside from the troops who try to follow them through the breaches.

The janissaries trapped inside fight [Michael Szilágyi](@belgrade-assault-szilagyi)’s men in the upper town and are killed. The troops outside the walls are beaten off with heavy losses. In the Turkish accounts the men who break in start to plunder and are caught by crusaders who lie in wait for them.
