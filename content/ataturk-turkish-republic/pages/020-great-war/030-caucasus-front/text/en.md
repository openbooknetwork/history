Promoted to brigadier general and pasha in April 1916, he takes the 16th Corps to the eastern front, where the Russians have taken Erzurum, Muş and Bitlis. On 6 to 8 August his corps retakes Muş and Bitlis, the only Ottoman successes on that front. Then a winter in Diyarbakır, a corps in the Hejaz he refuses, and the Second Army.
