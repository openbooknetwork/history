Gedik Ahmed Pasha, now a vizier, takes [Alanya](@alanya-1471) (then Alaiye) on the Mediterranean coast in 1471. In 1472 he takes [Silifke](@silifke-1472), Mokan and [Kızkalesi](@corycus-1472) (then Corycus) from the Karaman princes. Pope Sixtus IV spends more than 144,000 florins on a fleet to fight the Ottomans and to help Uzun Hasan, and in summer 1472 it gathers at [Rhodes](@rhodes-1472) under Cardinal Oliviero Carafa: 87 galleys, 46 of them Venetian under Pietro Mocenigo, 24 papal and 17 Neapolitan, with 15 ships of the Knights and 8,000 men.

@image alanya-red-tower-a9b40e

In August 1472 the fleet reaches Antalya. Early in the morning Vittore Soranzo’s ten galleys break the chain across the harbour, and the crews plunder the market and burn the warehouses by the quay. Stefano Malipiero leads the attack on the walls, but the ladders are too short, and in another account the attackers take the outer wall and find a second wall behind it. The fleet has no heavy guns, and the next day it withdraws after laying waste to the suburbs.

@image pietro-mocenigo-tomb-e2e034

Mocenigo’s fleet then sails north and destroys [İzmir](@smyrna-1472) (then Smyrna). With the help of the Venetian fleet, Kasım, a brother of Pir Ahmed, holds Silifke and the coast of Karaman for a while.
