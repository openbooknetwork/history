Mehmed prepares through the winter of 1472, and the army gathers at [Yenişehir](@yenisehir-1473) near Bursa, where the troops of Rumelia arrive by way of Gallipoli. Mehmed reaches Yenişehir on 11 April 1473 by one account, and an Italian letter of that year has him leave [Constantinople](@istanbul-1453) on 13 April. His sons join him with their troops, Mustafa from [Konya](@konya-1472) as governor of Karaman and Bayezid from [Amasya](@amasya-1473), and the army counts about 85,000 men. Besides the guns of the janissaries it carries light mortars, cast for the first time for this campaign.

Past [Sivas](@sivas-1473) the army enters high mountains and is caught in a snowstorm. For more than 40 days there is no news of Uzun Hasan, until his men attack Ottoman raiders at Niksar. The people of Koyulhisar and [Şebinkarahisar](@sebinkarahisar-1473) flee into the mountains, and at [Erzincan](@erzincan-1473) Turahanoğlu Ömer Bey with 5,000 men defeats an Aq Qoyunlu force of the same size.

At [Tercan](@tercan-1473) many of the people who have fled come down and surrender. The Ottomans march up the Euphrates, and Uzun Hasan's army appears on the far bank. Uzun Hasan has added the men of the Karaman princes to his Turkoman army, most of whom are light horsemen, with foot soldiers who carry lances.
