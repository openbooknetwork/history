Timur gives the Anatolian beyliks back to their old dynasties. Karaman goes with [Konya](@konya-1403) to Mehmed, a son of the Alaeddin whom Bayezid had executed, Germiyan to Yakub at [Kütahya](@kutahya-1402), and Aydın, Saruhan and Menteşe to their former houses. İsfendiyar of Candar, who submits to Timur, is confirmed in [Kastamonu](@kastamonu-1403), Kalecik, Tosya and Çankırı.

Bayezid’s sons keep what Timur leaves them, as his vassals. Süleyman rules the lands in Europe from [Edirne](@edirne-1403), İsa takes [Bursa](@bursa-1403) and the north-west of Anatolia with Timur’s leave, and Mehmed holds [Amasya](@amasya-1403) with Tokat and Sivas.

Bayezid dies a captive in Timur’s camp at [Akşehir](@aksehir-1403) on 8 March 1403, of illness by the Timurid historians, by poison he took himself according to Âşıkpaşazâde and Oruç Bey. Ibn Arabshah tells of an iron cage, while Yazdi writes that Timur treated his captive with respect. Muhammad Sultan, Timur’s grandson and heir, dies of his wounds near Afyonkarahisar a few days later, and Timur leaves Anatolia that year. He dies at Otrar (Farab) on the Syr Darya on 17 or 18 February 1405, at the start of a campaign against China.
