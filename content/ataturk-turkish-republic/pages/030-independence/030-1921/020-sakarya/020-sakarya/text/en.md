The Greek army crosses the Sakarya on 23 August and attacks along a front of a hundred kilometres. Mustafa Kemal, who has broken a rib in a fall from his horse, commands from the village of Alagöz. His order sets the method: "There is no line of defence, there is a surface of defence, and that surface is the whole country." Hills change hands for days; Çal Dağı falls on 2 September and is retaken.

On the night of 12 to 13 September the Greek army, out of reserves and supply, withdraws west across the river. Twenty two days, the longest pitched battle of the war, and the last Greek offensive.
