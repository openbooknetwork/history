# Translator, this book's rules

The name keys of step 2 are the markers, zones, routes and labels of the story, and the names and sides of its
battles. A battle's strings sit in its folder's catalogue, which the command of step 1 lists. Step 4 also fills each
battle's strengths and casualties.

A person, place or state of `content/<story>/.plan/names.md` takes its form from `i18n/names/<lang>.yaml`. Report a
name with no entry there, and the `name-finder` looks it up.
