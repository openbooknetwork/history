New Turkish recruits keep arriving while every Greek unit is already in the line, and for a few days neither exhausted army can attack. On 8 September Mustafa Kemal takes personal command of a counterattack against the Greek left around Çal Dağı. It gains little ground, but the Greek command fears a larger move round its flank as winter approaches.

On the night of 12 to 13 September the Greek army breaks off and withdraws across the Sakarya, taking its guns and equipment. Railways and bridges are blown up and villages burnt behind it. The Turks retake Sivrihisar on 20 September.
