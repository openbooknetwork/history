In late June 1920, with British approval, the Greek army advances out of the İzmir zone. It takes Balıkesir and, on 8 July, Bursa, while British troops occupy Mudanya on the Sea of Marmara. Further south Greek columns move up the Gediz and Büyük Menderes valleys towards Alaşehir and Uşak, which falls at the end of August.
