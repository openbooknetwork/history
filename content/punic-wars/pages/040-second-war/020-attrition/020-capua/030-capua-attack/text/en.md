In 211 BC [Hannibal](@capua-attack-hannibal) marches back from the south and camps close to the Roman lines. He attacks the outer line while the Carthaginian garrison of Capua under [Bostar and Hanno](@capua-attack-sortie), with the Capuans, makes a sortie against the inner one.

An [Iberian cohort](@capua-attack-cohort) with three elephants breaks through the outer line and reaches the rampart of a Roman camp, but is destroyed before it can break in. Livy writes that [Fulvius Flaccus](@capua-attack-fulvius) holds off Hannibal while [Appius Claudius](@capua-attack-appius) drives the Capuans back to their gates and is wounded there. The attack on the lines fails.
