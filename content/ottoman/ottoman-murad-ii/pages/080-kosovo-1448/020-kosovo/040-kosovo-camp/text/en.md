On 20 October the [Wallachians](@kosovo-camp-wallachians) go over to Murad on terms that he offers them, and Hunyadi is left with too few men to hold the field. The historian Emanuel Antoche thinks that the sources exaggerate the desertion.

With [Murad](@kosovo-camp-murad) watching, the janissaries attack the crusader stockade and kill those they find in it. Through the night the Ottoman foot shoots into the [wagon fort](@kosovo-camp-wagons), and the Hungarians answer with their guns. On the next day a last assault destroys what is left of the army. [Hunyadi](@kosovo-camp-hunyadi) escapes from the field with a few men, by Turkish accounts under cover of his wagons.

The accounts give very different losses. Laonikos Chalkokondyles writes of 17,000 crusaders and 4,000 Ottomans killed, Antonio Bonfini of 8,000 crusaders and 30,000 Ottomans, and Franz Babinger gives about 17,000 crusaders and 34,000 Ottomans. Franko Talovac, who led the Croatian banner at Varna, is among the dead.
