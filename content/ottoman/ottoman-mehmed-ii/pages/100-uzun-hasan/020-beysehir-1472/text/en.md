After Mehmed takes Konya and Larende in 1468, the Karaman princes Pir Ahmed and his brother Kasım flee to Uzun Hasan. Uzun Hasan makes an alliance with Venice, which is at war with Mehmed, and keeps in touch with the Knights of Rhodes and the king of Cyprus. In 1472 his cousin Yusuf Bayandur, Amir Beg of the Mawsillu tribe, Kızıl Ahmed of the Candar family and the Karaman princes ride into the Ottoman lands and sack [Tokat](@tokat-1472).

The raiders go on into Karaman, where Pir Ahmed takes back the town of [Karaman](@larende-1472) (then Larende) and becomes bey again. Mehmed's son Mustafa, the governor at [Konya](@konya-1472), and Gedik Ahmed Pasha meet them at Kıreli near Lake Beyşehir on 14 August 1472 and defeat them. Pir Ahmed flees once more, and Gedik Ahmed Pasha sends his family to Constantinople.

@image lake-beysehir-2a2ae3

Uzun Hasan now turns against the Mamluks as well. In November 1472 his son Uğurlu Mehmed attacks the Mamluk fortress of [Birecik](@birecik-1472) on the Euphrates, but he withdraws when the Mamluk emir Yashbak min Mahdi marches against him. Through the winter Mehmed prepares a campaign against Uzun Hasan, and, against his habit of keeping his plans secret, he writes to Uzun Hasan that he will march against him in the spring.
