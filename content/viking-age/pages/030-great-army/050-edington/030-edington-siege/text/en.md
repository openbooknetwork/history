The Danish line breaks, and by Asser’s account Alfred destroys the Vikings with great slaughter. He pursues those who flee as far as their stronghold, cutting them down, and seizes everything he finds outside it, men, horses and cattle, killing the men. Then he camps with his whole army before the gates.

After fourteen days the Danes, worn down by hunger, cold and fear, ask for peace. They give Alfred as many hostages as he wants and take none in return, swear to leave his kingdom and promise that Guthrum will be baptised. Tradition places the stronghold at Bratton Camp, whose double ramparts enclose about 9 hectares, while other historians take it to be the Danish base at Chippenham.
