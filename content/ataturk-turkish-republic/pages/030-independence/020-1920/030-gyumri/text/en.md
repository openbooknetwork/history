Kâzım Karabekir's Eastern Army moves against the Armenian republic in late September, takes Sarıkamış and on 30 October Kars. The Treaty of Gümrü, signed on 3 December 1920, is the Ankara government's first international agreement. It fixes the eastern border and frees the eastern divisions for the west.
