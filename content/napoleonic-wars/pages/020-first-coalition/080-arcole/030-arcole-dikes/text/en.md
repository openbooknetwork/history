On 16 November Alvinczi sends Provera with six battalions from Belfiore and Mittrowsky with 14 battalions south from Arcole, to meet at the French bridgehead at dawn. Provera runs into Masséna, General Brabeck is killed, and the Austrians are chased back to Belfiore with the loss of five guns. Mittrowsky and Augereau fight back and forth through the morning until the Austrians fall back to Arcole.

Mittrowsky places four battalions under Colonel Sticker on the western dike of the Alpone, lines the eastern dike with four battalions under Brigido and packs the rest of his men into Arcole. Bonaparte’s repeated attacks on the village fail all day, and so do his attempts to cross the Adige at Albaredo, which two Austrian battalions now guard, and the Alpone near its mouth. At nightfall Masséna and Augereau fall back towards the bridgehead, but strong forces stay on the Austrian side of the Adige.
