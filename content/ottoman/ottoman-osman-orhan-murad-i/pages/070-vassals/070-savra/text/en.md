Balša II, lord of Zeta from 1378, rules from [Scutari (Shkodër)](@scutari) and has held [Vlorë](@vlore), Berat and Kaninë through his wife since 1372. By 1385, at his fourth attempt, he has taken [Durrës](@durres) from Karl Thopia, the lord of central Albania, and he styles himself duke of Durazzo. On 8 August 1385 he asks Venice for four galleys against the Ottomans. Thopia calls on Murad for help.

Çandarlı Hayreddin Pasha leads his troops from the region of Ohrid to the Savra field near Lushnjë. Balša hears of the raid at Durrës. By the account of Mavro Orbini he gathers 1,000 men there and rides out against the advice of his nobles, and on 18 September 1385 he is killed and his head is taken to Hayreddin. Orbini’s history is the main source for the battle and contains many errors.

Thopia takes Durrës back, probably as an Ottoman vassal, and most of the Serbian and Albanian lords of the region accept Ottoman overlordship. The Ottomans take Krujë, Berat and Ulcinj but soon leave them, keeping only [Kastoria](@kastoria-1385). Balša’s nephew Đurađ II Balšić succeeds him in Zeta.
