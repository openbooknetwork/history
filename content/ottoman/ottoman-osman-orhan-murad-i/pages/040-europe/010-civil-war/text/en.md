Andronikos III dies in June 1341, and his son John V is nine years old. John Kantakouzenos, the late emperor’s chief minister, acts as regent. In September, while he is away from [Constantinople](@constantinople), the megas doux Alexios Apokaukos and Patriarch John XIV win over the empress Anna of Savoy and take power. In October 1341 Kantakouzenos’s army proclaims him emperor at [Didymoteicho](@didymoteicho), and the two sides go to war.

Most towns of Thrace and Macedonia side with the regency, and the Zealots take over [Thessalonica](@thessalonica). In the winter of 1342–1343 Umur of Aydın sails up the Maritsa with a large fleet, relieves Didymoteicho and fights for Kantakouzenos until 1344, when a crusading fleet takes the harbour castle of [Smyrna](@smyrna-1344) and draws him home. Stefan Dušan of Serbia, first Kantakouzenos’s ally and then the regency’s, takes most of Macedonia, Serres in September 1345, and is crowned emperor at [Skopje](@skopje) on 16 April 1346.

From 1345 Orhan sends troops to Kantakouzenos. In the summer of 1346 Orhan marries Kantakouzenos’s daughter Theodora: his envoys come to Selymbria with 30 ships and take her across the Marmara to Bithynia. On the night of 2 February 1347 Kantakouzenos’s men enter Constantinople by the Golden Gate, and on 8 February he becomes senior emperor beside John V. The Black Death reaches the city the same year.
