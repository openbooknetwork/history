The Ottomans draw up with the Anatolian troops of [Mehmed](@kosovo-flanks-mehmed) on the right wing and the Rumelian sipahis on the left. In the centre [Murad](@kosovo-flanks-murad) stands with the janissaries and the azaps behind a ditch. Turkish accounts put a line of shield bearers and spearmen round the ditch against the crusader horse, with camels and guns along it. [John Hunyadi](@kosovo-flanks-hunyadi) holds the crusader centre with his heavy horse in front of the [wagon fort](@kosovo-flanks-wagons), and the [Wallachians](@kosovo-flanks-wallachians) form his left wing.

The battle opens on Thursday 17 October, when Hunyadi sends his horse, light and heavy, against both Ottoman wings. The sipahis of Rumelia and Anatolia give ground until the Ottoman light horse comes up to support them. Turkish accounts say instead that Murad keeps the Anatolian wing out of the first day’s fighting to rest it, and that the two sides spend the day testing each other’s strength.

On 18 October the horsemen fight again on the flanks of the two stockades. The fighting of the first two days costs both sides heavily and settles nothing.
