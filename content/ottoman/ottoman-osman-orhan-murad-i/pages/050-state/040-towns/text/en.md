[Bursa](@orhan-mosque) grows into the Ottoman capital. In 1339–1340 Orhan founds a mosque there as part of a complex with an imaret, a kitchen that feeds the poor, a medrese, a school, a bath and the Emir Han, a caravanserai in the bazaar whose rents pay for the rest. Like other early Ottoman mosques it also lodges dervishes. Ibn Battuta describes Bursa as a great city with fine bazaars and wide streets, surrounded by gardens and springs.

@image bursa-orhan-mosque-719317

In İznik Orhan founds the first Ottoman medrese in 1331, soon after the city surrenders. The Hacı Özbek Mosque of 1333, a square hall under a single dome, is the oldest dated Ottoman mosque, and its walls of brick and cut stone follow Byzantine methods of building. In 1388 Murad builds an imaret near the Lefke Gate in memory of his mother Nilüfer Hatun.

@image iznik-haci-ozbek-mosque-f2ea31

Between 1365 and 1385 Murad builds his own complex at Çekirge, west of Bursa, with a mosque, a medrese, a dervish lodge, an imaret and a bath. After the fall of [Adrianople](@edirne-palace) his court moves between Bursa, [Didymoteicho](@dimetoka-palace) and Edirne, where he builds palaces and mosques. Edirne becomes the main Ottoman base in the Balkans.
