Despot Jovan Uglješa of Serres seeks allies against the Ottomans. Byzantium and Bulgaria stay out, and only his brother, King Vukašin of Prilep, joins him. In the summer of 1371 Vukašin brings his army from Scutari (Shkodër) in Zeta, and while Murad is in Anatolia the brothers march east toward Edirne.

Their army camps by the Maritsa near Chernomen (Ormenio), about 30 km west of Edirne. Before dawn on 26 September 1371 Ottoman troops under Lala Şahin and Hacı İlbey fall on the camp, and Vukašin, Uglješa and many of their men are killed or drown in the river. The numbers are uncertain: the Serbian monk Isaiah writes of 60,000 Serbs, while modern historians think the Serbian army was probably under 10,000.

The Ottomans advance into Uglješa’s lands in Macedonia, and Vukašin’s son Marko and other Serbian lords become Ottoman vassals. Tsar Uroš V dies childless in December 1371, the last ruler of the Nemanjić dynasty, and the Serbian lands are divided among regional princes such as Lazar.
