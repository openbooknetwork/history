On 25 April 1915 the Allies land at Cape Helles and, with Australian and New Zealand troops, at Arıburnu. Mustafa Kemal's 19th Division is the reserve. He marches the 57th Regiment to the ridge above the landing on his own initiative and holds it through the day. The order attributed to him, "I do not order you to attack, I order you to die", belongs to this morning.
