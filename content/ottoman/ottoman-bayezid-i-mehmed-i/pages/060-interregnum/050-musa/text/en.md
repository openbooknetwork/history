In 1409 Mehmed and Musa meet near Kırşehir and agree that Musa will open a war against Süleyman in Europe. Musa sails from [Sinop](@sinop) with İsfendiyar’s help to Wallachia, where Mircea receives him, and raises an army of Wallachians and of Turkmen settled along the Danube and in Dobruja. Stefan Lazarević, whose brother Vuk had forced him that year with Süleyman’s troops to give up the lands south of the West Morava, allies with Musa.

Early in 1410 Musa crosses the Danube and beats Süleyman’s beylerbey at [Yambol](@yambol-1410). Süleyman hurries back from Anatolia and crosses at Constantinople with the help of Manuel II, while Mehmed takes [Bursa](@bursa-1410) behind him. On 15 June 1410 the brothers fight at Kosmidion, outside the land walls of Constantinople. Musa’s army has the upper hand until Süleyman attacks his camp with a few hundred men, and Vuk Lazarević and other lords who had joined Musa go over to Süleyman.

@image mircea-mihail-cozia-08043f

Musa withdraws to Yambol, his men capture Vuk at [Philippopolis](@philippopolis-1410), and Musa has him executed. Süleyman beats Musa again near [Edirne](@edirne-1411) on 11 July, but loses supporters over the winter. On 17 February 1411 Musa takes Edirne by surprise, and Süleyman, fleeing toward Constantinople, is caught and killed.
