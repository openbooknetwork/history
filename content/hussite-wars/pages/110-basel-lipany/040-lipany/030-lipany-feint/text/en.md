[Mikuláš Krchlebec](@lipany-feint-krchlebec) of Krchleby, burgrave of Zvíkov, leads the league’s vanguard on foot towards the field armies’ fort. It fires its four guns at the front of the fort, halts as if in confusion and draws the fire of all the field armies’ guns. Then it turns and falls back towards its own wagons as if beaten.

A wagon fort holds as long as its wagons stay chained: horsemen cannot ride through them, and men on foot who climb them meet flails and handguns. The feigned retreat brings the defenders out. Taking it for a real flight, the field armies unchain their wagons and pursue onto the plain, horse and foot, shouting “They are running!”. Bartošek of Drahonice has the whole fort move after the league. Most modern historians hold that a detachment went out with a few wagons from the head of the column and left the fort open behind it.
