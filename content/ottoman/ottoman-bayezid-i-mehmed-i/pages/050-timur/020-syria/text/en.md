From Sivas Timur marches south, takes [Malatya](@malatya-1400) and enters Mamluk Syria. The Mamluk army of Syria gathers at Aleppo, and Timur defeats it and sacks the town in October or November 1400. Hama, Homs and Baalbek surrender, and in December he lays siege to [Damascus](@damascus-1401).

@image damascus-zafarnama-bfbef5

The Mamluk sultan Faraj comes from Cairo with an army, but for fear of a revolt in Egypt he leaves it in Syria and hurries home. The historian Ibn Khaldun, who has come with him, stays in the besieged town for seven weeks. He is lowered over the wall on ropes to treat with Timur and meets him several times early in 1401. Damascus surrenders and is plundered, the Umayyad Mosque burns in March 1401, and many of the town’s craftsmen are taken to Samarkand.

@image ibn-khaldun-hand-0b98cf

Timur marches north again by Mardin and lays siege to [Baghdad](@baghdad-1401), the seat of Ahmad Jalayir. He storms it after forty days in the summer of 1401, has much of its population killed and winters again in [Karabagh](@karabagh). While he is away, Bayezid marches on Erzincan, takes Taharten captive and then leaves him the town as a vassal. He keeps the castle of [Kemah](@kemah-1401) with an Ottoman garrison and sends Taharten’s family to Bursa as hostages. Timur demands Kemah and Taharten’s family back, Qara Yusuf dead or alive, and one of Bayezid’s sons as a hostage.
