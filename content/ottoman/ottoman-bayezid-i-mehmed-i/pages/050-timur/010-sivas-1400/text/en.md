Timur, a Turco-Mongol emir from Transoxiana, has ruled from Samarkand since 1370. He has conquered Persia, Iraq and the Caucasus, beaten the Golden Horde in 1391 and 1395 and sacked Delhi in 1398. In September 1399 he sets out on a new campaign in the west and spends the winter in [Karabagh](@karabagh). In the spring of 1400 he invades [Georgia](@tbilisi-1400) and defeats King George VII.

At [Pasinler](@pasinler-1400) the beys whom Bayezid has driven from their lands, among them Yakub of Germiyan and İlyas of Menteşe, come to Timur and ask him to restore them. Timur also wins over Kara Yülük of the Aq Qoyunlu and Taharten, the emir of [Erzincan](@erzincan-1399), from whom Bayezid has demanded tribute. Ahmad Jalayir of Baghdad and Qara Yusuf of the Kara Koyunlu, whom Timur has driven out, take refuge with Bayezid, who gives them Kütahya and Aksaray as fiefs. The two rulers exchange letters full of threats and insults.

@image ahmad-jalayir-1396-4e2093

In August 1400 Timur takes Sivas, the Ottoman fortress on the upper Kızılırmak, and burns the town. He lets those who surrender go and has about 4,000 of the defenders buried alive. Sharaf ad-Din Ali Yazdi and Nizam ad-Din Shami write that they were Christians, most of them Armenians, and by one tradition Bayezid’s son Ertuğrul dies in the town. From Sivas Timur turns south against the Mamluks.
