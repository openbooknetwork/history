After Darius III dies in summer 330 BC,\* stabbed by his own satraps, Alexander crosses the Elburz into Hyrcania on the Caspian shore. At [Zadracarta](@zadracarta-330) (perhaps today's Sari) Darius's officers, the loyal Artabazus among them, and his 1,500 Greek mercenaries surrender.

Alexander marches east through Parthia to [Susia](@susia-330), probably today's Tus, where Satibarzanes, the satrap of Areia, submits. Then news comes from [Bactra](@bactra-330) (today Balkh): Bessus, the Bactrian satrap who led the attack on Darius, has put on the upright tiara of the Persian kings as King Artaxerxes. Alexander answers, in Bosworth's reading, as Darius's true heir. He dresses his senior Companions in Persian courtiers' scarlet and adds the Great King's diadem, girdle and white-striped tunic to his own Macedonian hat and cloak. Many Macedonians resent it, as Arrian does centuries later.

@image bessus-supply-list-aramaic-648945

As the king sets out for Bactria, Satibarzanes kills the 40 Macedonian horsemen left with him and declares for Bessus. Alexander turns back and, Arrian says, covers 600 stades, about 110 km, in two days to [Artacoana](@artacoana-330), the Areian capital, and Satibarzanes flees. The army then halts at [Phrada](@phrada-330) (today Farah) in Drangiana, whose satrap Barsaentes has fled.

There a plot against the king surfaces. Philotas, son of the old general Parmenion, had been told of it and said nothing. He is tried before the Macedonians and put to death. Riders on racing camels carry the king's orders across the desert to [Ecbatana](@ecbatana-330b), where the officers Cleander, Sitalces and Menidas kill Parmenion. Arrian suggests that Parmenion, guilty or not, was too dangerous to outlive his son. Bosworth sees a king bent on destroying the family and tired of the old man's quarrels over policy.

Hyrcania, Parthia, Areia and Drangiana join the empire, and Bessus holds Bactria and Sogdiana.

### Notes

\* Arrian dates Darius's death to July 330 BC. Bosworth finds the march from Persis too long for that and places it in August.
