In spring 146 BC Scipio launches the final assault from the harbour area. Hasdrubal, expecting it, sets fire to the buildings around the merchant harbour. A Roman party, led by Gaius Laelius according to Appian, climbs the wall of the naval harbour and takes it, and the main force reaches the city’s main square, where the legions camp for the night.

The next morning Scipio brings up 4,000 fresh men to join the troops at the naval harbour. On the way they stop to strip the gold from the temple of Apollo, and Scipio and his officers cannot prevent it. The Carthaginians do not counterattack. They fall back to the streets below the citadel on the Byrsa hill.
