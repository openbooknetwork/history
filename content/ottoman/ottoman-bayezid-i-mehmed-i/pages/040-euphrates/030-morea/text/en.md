Between September 1395 and January 1396 Theodore I, the despot of the Morea at [Mistra](@mistra), buys [Corinth](@corinth-1397) and Megara from Carlo Tocco. After Nicopolis the Ottomans turn to Greece. In 1397 an army under Yakub Pasha and Evrenos plunders the villages of Boeotia and of Attica around Venetian [Athens](@athens-1394). It crosses the Isthmus and in June takes the Venetian town of [Argos](@argos-1394). At least 14,000 of its people are enslaved, and many are taken to Anatolia. Raiders plunder the lands of the Venetian ports of [Coron](@coron-1397) and [Modon](@modon-1397).

Theodore cannot rebuild the walls of Corinth or defend it, and in 1397 he sells the town to the Knights Hospitaller of Rhodes. New Ottoman attacks on the Morea follow in 1399 and 1400, and in 1400 Theodore sells the whole despotate to the Knights. When the Knights’ men come to take over Mistra, its people refuse to receive them.
