Sidelined by the Committee's leaders, Mustafa Kemal is sent to Sofia as military attaché. He watches a new Balkan state at work, follows the outbreak of the European war, and argues against the empire joining Germany too early. When it does, he asks for a front command and is given the 19th Division at Tekirdağ in January 1915.
