İbrahim II of Karaman dies in 1464, and his sons fight over his lands. İshak, his heir, governs at [Silifke](@silifke-1464), while his younger son Pir Ahmed, whose mother is a daughter of Mehmed I, declares himself bey at [Konya](@konya-1468). İshak takes the throne with the help of Uzun Hasan of the Aq Qoyunlu, and Pir Ahmed asks Mehmed for help and offers him part of the beylik in return.

@image konya-alaeddin-hill-c9eadc

In 1465 Pir Ahmed defeats İshak with Ottoman troops at [Dağpazarı](@dagpazari-1465), becomes bey and hands over the promised land, but he takes it back while Mehmed is away in Albania. In April 1468, by Faruk Sümer’s dating, Mehmed’s army takes the castle of [Gevele](@gevele-1468) and then Konya, and Mehmed goes on to the town of [Karaman](@larende-1468) (then Larende), while Pir Ahmed escapes to the east. Many families of Konya and Larende are moved to Constantinople, and in 1468 Mahmud Pasha loses the office of grand vizier, officially over the way that this resettlement is handled.

@image karaman-citadel-bcfcf9

Mehmed leaves his son Mustafa as governor at Konya. In 1469 Gedik Ahmed Pasha takes [Aksaray](@aksaray-1469) and [Ereğli](@eregli-1469), and Pir Ahmed keeps only the castles of the Taurus, such as [Ermenek](@ermenek-1469), and the coast below them.
