The armistice is signed aboard HMS Agamemnon at Mudros on 30 October 1918. The next day Mustafa Kemal takes over the Yıldırım Army Group from Liman von Sanders. He objects to the terms, among them the article that lets the Allies occupy any strategic point if their security is threatened. The army group is dissolved in early November, and he arrives in İstanbul on 13 November, the day the Allied fleet anchors off the city.
