On 6 February 886 the Seine, swollen by rain and choked with the debris of the siege, rises and breaks the Petit Pont. The tower at the left bank end of the bridge is cut off from the city with twelve men inside.

The Danes attack the tower from the left bank and set it on fire, and the twelve defenders are killed. Abbo records the names of all twelve. Part of the Danish army then leaves the siege to raid Le Mans, Chartres and Évreux, while a force stays in the camps before the city.
