İsfendiyar, the Candar bey at [Kastamonu](@kastamonu-1417) and [Sinop](@sinop), keeps the peace with Mehmed. In 1417, or 1416 by another account, his son Kasım rises against him with Mehmed’s backing, and İsfendiyar has to give up the lands south of the Ilgaz mountains. Kasım holds [Çankırı](@cankiri-1417) and [Tosya](@tosya-1417) under the sultan.

After Ankara, Samsun passed through the hands of local lords to the Candar beys, and İsfendiyar gave it to his younger son Hızır. Beside the Muslim town stood the walled town of the Genoese, as Clavijo saw it in 1403. In 1420 Mehmed sets out from [Tokat](@tokat-1420), takes his son Murad and Murad’s tutor Hamza Bey with him at [Amasya](@amasya-1420), and sends Hamza ahead to Samsun. The Genoese set their town on fire and sail away, and Hızır hands the keys of the castle to Mehmed in the weeks after 11 August 1420, or in 1419 by another account.
