In August 1918 Mustafa Kemal returns to Palestine as commander of the Seventh Army. On 19 September Allenby's offensive at Megiddo breaks the Ottoman line. Mustafa Kemal brings what is left of his army north through Damascus to [Aleppo](@aleppo) and, on 26 October, holds a line at Qatma north of the city, close to the present Turkish–Syrian border.
