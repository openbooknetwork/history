In the spring of 1403 Mehmed, Timur’s vassal at [Amasya](@amasya-1403), proposes to İsa at [Bursa](@bursa-mehmed-1403) that they divide Anatolia between them. İsa refuses, saying that as the elder brother he is entitled to the whole. Mehmed marches west and defeats him at Ulubad between March and early May 1403.

İsa flees to [Constantinople](@constantinople) and goes on to Süleyman at [Edirne](@edirne-1403). Mehmed enters Bursa, holds an enthronement there, has his father buried in the town and strikes coins bearing Timur’s name with his own. By 18 May İsa is back in Anatolia with troops from Süleyman, but he fails to take Bursa.

Supported by Cüneyd of İzmir and other beys whom Timur had restored, İsa fights on, but Mehmed beats him again in [Karasi](@balikesir-1403) and he flees to Karaman. He is later caught in a bath at [Eskişehir](@eskisehir-1403) and strangled by Mehmed’s men. By the report of Clavijo he is dead by September 1403, while older Turkish histories place his death in 1406. İsa is buried beside his father at Bursa.
