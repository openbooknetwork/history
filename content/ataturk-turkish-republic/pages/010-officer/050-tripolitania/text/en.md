When Italy invades Ottoman Libya in 1911, the empire cannot send an army by sea. Young officers go on their own, through Egypt in disguise. Mustafa Kemal reaches Tobruk in December 1911 and commands at Derna from March 1912, organising local Arab fighters against the Italian beachheads. The Balkan War pulls him home in October 1912.
