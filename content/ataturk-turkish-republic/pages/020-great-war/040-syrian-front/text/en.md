In August 1918 he returns to Palestine as commander of the Seventh Army. On 19 September Allenby's offensive at Megiddo breaks the Ottoman line. He brings what is left of his army north through Damascus to Aleppo and, on 26 October, holds a line at Katma north of the city, close to the present Turkish–Syrian border.
