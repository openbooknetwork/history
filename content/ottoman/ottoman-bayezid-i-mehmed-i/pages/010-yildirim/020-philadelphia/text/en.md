After Murad’s death the beys of Anatolia move against the Ottomans. Alaeddin Ali of Karaman, Bayezid’s brother-in-law, takes [Beyşehir](@beysehir-1390) again and raids as far as Eskişehir. Yakub II of Germiyan claims back the lands his sister brought Bayezid as her dowry, and Kadı Burhaneddin of Sivas takes Kırşehir.

Bayezid crosses to Anatolia in 1390 with Serbian vassal troops. In one summer campaign he takes Germiyan, Saruhan with its seat at [Manisa](@manisa-1390), Aydın at [Ayasluğ](@ayasluk-1390) and Menteşe at [Milas](@milas-1390), and holds Yakub of Germiyan prisoner. The Hamid lands around [Eğirdir](@egirdir-1390) follow in 1390, or 1391 by other accounts. Bayezid retakes Beyşehir and besieges Alaeddin in [Konya](@konya-1390), but when Süleyman Bey of Candar and Kadı Burhaneddin move to help Karaman, he makes peace, and Alaeddin gives up his lands west of the Çarşamba river.

John VII, son of Andronikos IV, claims the throne of his grandfather John V. With the backing of Bayezid and the Genoese he marches from Selymbria and enters [Constantinople](@constantinople) on 14 April 1390, when townspeople open the Gate of Charisius to him. John V’s son Manuel returns with galleys of the Knights of Rhodes and drives him out on 17 September. Bayezid then calls both Manuel and John VII to serve in his army, and they are with it when Philadelphia, the last Byzantine town in Anatolia, surrenders to the sultan.
