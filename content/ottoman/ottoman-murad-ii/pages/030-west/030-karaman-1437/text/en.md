While Murad is at war in Europe, Ibrahim II of Karaman, who rules from [Konya](@konya-1437), retakes [Beyşehir](@beysehir-1437) and the old Hamid lands from the Ottomans and makes a secret alliance with Hungary. Murad returns to Anatolia in 1433, and Ibrahim asks for peace, which is made in 1435.

@image karaman-tiled-mihrab-e39b1c

In the east, Shah Rukh, the son of Timur, campaigns against the Kara Koyunlu in 1434, and in 1435–1436 he deposes their ruler İskender, who takes refuge with the Ottomans and spends the winter of 1435–1436 at [Tokat](@tokat-1435). Shah Rukh returns to Khorasan in 1436. Shortly after the peace, Ibrahim lays siege to [Amasya](@amasya-1437), and Murad answers by backing the Dulkadirids, who take [Kayseri](@kayseri-1436) from Karaman, and Ibrahim’s brother İsa, who takes [Akşehir](@aksehir-1437).

In 1437 Murad marches into Karaman and takes back Beyşehir and the Hamid lands. Ibrahim lifts the siege of Amasya and makes a new peace. Murad’s eldest son Ahmed, the governor of Amasya, dies in 1437, or 1438 by other accounts. By some accounts Murad sends his son Mehmed, born in 1432, to Amasya in his place.
