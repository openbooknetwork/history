In 1423 Murad marches against İsfendiyar of Candar, who had backed Küçük Mustafa. He takes much of İsfendiyar’s land, with Taraklıborlu, today [Safranbolu](@safranbolu-1423), and İsfendiyar withdraws to [Sinop](@sinop). By the peace that follows, İsfendiyar keeps Kastamonu and Sinop, and in 1425, or 1424 by other accounts, Murad marries İsfendiyar’s daughter Hatice Halime Hatun.

Cüneyd, who had been given the governorship of Aydın after Ulubad, tries to win back his old beylik. He seeks help from Venice and Karaman and backs a new pretender, Prince İsmail, whom Murad’s Genoese allies keep from sailing to Rumelia. The governor of Aydın, Yahşi Bey, and the beylerbey of Anatolia, Oruç Bey, cannot subdue him. In 1425 Hamza Bey, Bayezid Pasha’s brother and the new beylerbey, beats Cüneyd near [Akhisar](@akhisar-1425), and Cüneyd shuts himself up in the castle of İpsili on the coast opposite Samos.

Genoese ships from [Chios](@chios) and Lesbos keep help from reaching Cüneyd by sea. Cüneyd slips away to Ibrahim of Karaman and comes back with Karaman troops, but they leave him when they reach İpsili, and he surrenders to Hamza Bey. Cüneyd and his son Bayezid are killed, and Aydın is annexed. Menteşe, whose bey İlyas had died in 1424, is annexed in 1426 with its seat at Milas. Teke, whose bey Osman Çelebi dies in 1423, is annexed that year, or in 1425 by other accounts.
