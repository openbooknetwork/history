Alexander's charge reaches the guard about Darius. [The guard](@issus-rout-guard) breaks, and [Darius III](@issus-rout-darius) flees, first in his chariot and then, where the ground grows rough, on horseback.\* Arsames, who had held Cilicia, is among the Persian nobles killed.

On the right the Macedonians turn against the Greek mercenaries in the river, take them in the flank and drive them from the bank.\*\* By the sea the Persian cavalry fights on until it learns that Darius has fled and the mercenaries are cut off. Then it breaks. The heavily armed horsemen crowd into the narrow roads and trample one another, and the Thessalians under [Parmenion](@issus-rout-parmenion) pursue them. [Alexander](@issus-rout-alexander) rides after Darius as long as there is daylight. Arrian, following Ptolemy, son of Lagus, who was there, says the pursuers crossed a ravine on the bodies of the dead. [Darius's camp](@issus-rout-camp) is taken at the first assault, with his mother, his wife and his children.

The ancient authors put the Persian dead at 100,000 or more and the Macedonian dead at a few hundred.\*\*\* The historian A. B. Bosworth calls these figures propaganda, but holds that the Persian army was destroyed in its flight more than in the fighting.

The places of the pursuing and fleeing bodies, the directions of Darius's flight and of the cavalry's, and the place of the camp are not given by the sources.

### Notes

\* Arrian has Darius flee among the first as soon as his left is routed. Curtius and Diodorus have his brother lead the cavalry in front of the chariot and fall back only after a hard fight, until the wounded horses begin to throw the king from the chariot. Bosworth thinks that Darius held out longer than Arrian allows, though the fight around him was short.

\*\* Arrian says the battalions of the right wheeled onto the mercenaries' flank. Bosworth gives the push to Alexander's cavalry moving towards the sea.

\*\*\* For the Persian dead, Arrian gives about 100,000, more than 10,000 of them horsemen. Diodorus gives more than 100,000 foot and at least 10,000 horse besides, and Curtius 100,000 foot and 10,000 horse. For the Macedonians, Diodorus gives 300 foot and 150 horse killed, and Curtius 302 foot missing, 150 horse killed and about 4,500 wounded. Arrian names no total, but says about 120 Macedonians of rank fell in the centre.
