The Greek army's full summer offensive opens on 10 July 1921 with superior numbers. Kütahya falls, then Eskişehir on 19 July. Mustafa Kemal orders İsmet to break contact and pull the whole Western Front back behind the Sakarya river, giving up ground to keep the army. Ankara prepares to move its government to Kayseri.

On 5 August the Assembly passes the commander in chief law, which gives Mustafa Kemal its own powers for three months. He issues the "national obligations" orders: each household must give the army a share of its food and cloth, and one set of underwear and a pair of sandals.
