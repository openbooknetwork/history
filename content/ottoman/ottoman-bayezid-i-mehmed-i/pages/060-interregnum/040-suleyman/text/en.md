Süleyman crosses into Anatolia with a large army and takes [Bursa](@bursa-1404) in March 1404, and Mehmed falls back to [Amasya](@amasya-1403). Süleyman marches to the Aegean coast, where Cüneyd of [İzmir](@izmir-1404) and İlyas of Menteşe accept him as overlord. Later in 1404 [Ankara](@ankara-1404) surrenders to him, by one account after his grand vizier Çandarlı Ali Pasha sends its garrison forged letters from Mehmed.

Mehmed keeps Amasya, Tokat and the province of Rum. Musa, whom Timur had left in the keeping of Yakub of Germiyan at [Kütahya](@kutahya-1402), is released at Mehmed’s request and joins him.

@image amasya-panorama-99eebf

The division lasts from 1405 to 1410: Süleyman rules the Balkans from [Edirne](@edirne-1403) and western Anatolia from Bursa, and Mehmed the north-east from Amasya. Ali Pasha, grand vizier since 1387, dies at Ankara on 18 December 1406.
