On 10 August King Constantine commits the army to an attack on the Sakarya line. The Greek divisions march east for nine days through country short of food and water, and swing round to the south to turn the Turkish left. On 23 August they make contact with the advanced Turkish positions south of the Gök river.

The Turkish front follows the hills on the east bank of the Sakarya from near Polatlı south to the mouth of the Gök, then turns east along the Gök. The river loop in front of it has steep banks and only two bridges. The general staff has its headquarters at Polatlı, on the railway, and Mustafa Kemal his command post at Alagöz.
