Fifteen years as president: the caliphate abolished in 1924, the hat and the civil code in 1925 and 1926, the Latin alphabet in 1928, the surname law in 1934 that makes him Atatürk, the vote for women the same year. He dies at Dolmabahçe Palace in İstanbul on 10 November 1938 at five past nine in the morning.
