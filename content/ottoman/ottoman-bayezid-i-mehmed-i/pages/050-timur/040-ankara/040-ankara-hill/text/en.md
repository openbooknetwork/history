In the afternoon [Mehmed Çelebi](@ankara-hill-mehmed) leaves the field with his troops for Amasya, and İsa leaves too. The Ottoman chronicles lose track of Mustafa in the fighting, while Timur’s letter of victory and Yazdi have him taken prisoner. With both wings gone, Bayezid falls back with a few thousand janissaries to the hill Bıyıktay calls Çataltepe, about 4 km behind his line.

Timur sends his son [Shah Rukh](@ankara-hill-shahrukh) with Miran Shah and other commanders to take Bayezid. They surround the hill, and the janissaries drive back their attacks until the evening. At sunset Bayezid breaks out to the north-east with about 300 horsemen, many of whom die of heat and thirst.

The Chagatai khan Sultan Mahmud, who rides in Timur’s army, overtakes [Bayezid](@ankara-hill-bayezid) at Mahmutoğlan, about 16 km from the hill, when his horse stumbles, and takes him after a blow from a mace. Kemalpaşazade writes that Yakub of Germiyan recognised him. His son Musa is taken with him. Near midnight Bayezid is brought to the tent of [Timur](@ankara-hill-timur), who receives him with respect by Yazdi’s account.
