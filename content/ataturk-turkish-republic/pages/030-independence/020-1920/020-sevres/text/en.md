The İstanbul government signs the Treaty of Sèvres on 10 August 1920. It gives Thrace and the İzmir region to Greece, creates an Armenian state in the east, puts the straits under international control and carves spheres for France and Italy. The Assembly in Ankara rejects it and declares its signatories traitors. The treaty is never ratified.
