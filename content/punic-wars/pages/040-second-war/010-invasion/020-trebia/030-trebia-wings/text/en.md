Hannibal draws up 8,000 Gauls in the centre, with 6,000 Africans and Iberians on each side of them, half of his 30 elephants beyond each of these, and 5,000 cavalry on each wing. Sempronius has about 30,000 heavy infantry, the Roman legions in the centre and the allied legions on both sides, and 2,000 cavalry on each wing. The Balearic slingers and javelinmen outrange the tired, hungry velites and drive them back through the gaps in their own infantry.

On each wing 5,000 Carthaginian horsemen charge 2,000 Roman ones, whose horses are worn out from chasing the Numidians. Both Roman wings break at once and flee back over the Trebia, with most of the Carthaginian cavalry in pursuit. The Carthaginian light infantry move round to the open flanks of the Roman infantry, and the two main lines meet.
