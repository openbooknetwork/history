Mustafa is born in [Thessaloniki](@salonica) (then Salonica) in 1881. His father, Ali Rıza, is a minor official who later becomes a timber merchant, and his mother is Zübeyde. Thessaloniki is the largest port of the Ottoman Balkans, with Muslim, Jewish, Greek and Bulgarian communities and a railway to Europe.

@image salonica-birth-house-8a09b6

Mustafa is sent first to a Quran school in his neighbourhood, then to the modern Şemsi Efendi school. He is seven when his father dies.
