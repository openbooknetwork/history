In 1451 Ibrahim II of Karaman takes [Seydişehir](@seydisehir-1451) and [Akşehir](@aksehir-1451) from the Ottomans and stirs up the heirs of the beyliks that the Ottomans had annexed. Mehmed crosses to Anatolia in the summer and marches against him, and Ibrahim, who rules from [Konya](@konya-1451), submits and makes peace. It is the first campaign of Mehmed’s reign.

@image ibrahim-ii-karaman-dirhem-51ab00

On the march home the janissaries demand a gift for the new sultan’s accession, and Mehmed pays it. Every sultan after him pays the janissaries such a gift when he takes the throne.

@image janissary-bellini-4539b8

While Mehmed is in Anatolia, Constantine XI sends envoys to ask for more money for the upkeep of Orhan Çelebi, the only other living man of the Ottoman house, who lives as a hostage in [Constantinople](@constantinople). The envoys hint that Orhan may be set free to claim the throne. Doukas places the embassy in 1451, during the Karaman campaign, and Caroline Finkel places it in 1452. Halil receives the envoys at [Bursa](@bursa-1451) and sends them away with angry words, and back at [Edirne](@edirne) Mehmed stops the revenues set aside for Orhan.
