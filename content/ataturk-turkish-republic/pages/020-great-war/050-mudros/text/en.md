The armistice is signed at Mudros on 30 October 1918. The next day Mustafa Kemal takes over the Yıldırım Army Group from Liman von Sanders. He protests the armistice terms that let the Allies occupy any point they choose, is recalled, and arrives in İstanbul on 13 November, the same day the Allied fleet anchors off the city.
