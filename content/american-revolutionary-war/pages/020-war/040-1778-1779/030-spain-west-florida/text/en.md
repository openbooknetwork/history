By the Treaty of Aranjuez of April 1779 Spain joins France against Britain, and in June it declares war. It does not ally with the United States. Bernardo de Gálvez, the governor of Louisiana, moves against British West Florida from New Orleans. In September 1779 he takes Fort Bute at Manchac and Baton Rouge, and the British surrender Natchez with them.

On 14 March 1780 Gálvez takes Mobile. A larger expedition from Havana besieges Pensacola, the capital of West Florida, from March 1781, and its garrison surrenders on 10 May. West Florida passes to Spain, while East Florida, with St. Augustine, stays British.
