In late September 1920 Kâzım Karabekir's Eastern Front attacks the Armenian republic. It takes Sarıkamış, then [Kars](@kars) on 30 October and [Gyumri](@gyumri) (then Alexandropol) in November. The campaign frees the eastern divisions for the war in the west.

@image karabekir-1918-bf5b0f

The Treaty of Alexandropol is signed on 3 December 1920. Armenia gives up Kars and renounces the Treaty of Sèvres. The Armenian government has handed power to the Soviets the day before, and the Treaty of Alexandropol is never ratified. The Treaty of Kars fixes the border in October 1921.
