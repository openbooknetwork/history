That night, as his Iberian allies desert, Hasdrubal slips out of the camp and makes for the ocean. Scipio pursues with the cavalry in front and the legions behind and catches the column on the march. Hasdrubal is left with 6,000 men on a hilltop without water, who surrender soon after, while he and Mago escape to Gades.

Hasdrubal Gisco sails to Africa, to the court of Syphax, and Mago to the Balearic Islands, from where he later lands in Liguria. Gades goes over to Rome, and Masinissa, who led Carthage’s Numidian cavalry in Iberia, changes sides. Scipio settles his wounded at Italica near Ilipa, returns to Rome and is elected consul for 205 BC.
