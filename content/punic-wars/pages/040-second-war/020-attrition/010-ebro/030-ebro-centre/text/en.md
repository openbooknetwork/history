The skirmishers exchange missiles and fall back, and the two Roman legions advance on the Iberian infantry. After an exchange of javelins the Iberians break and flee almost without fighting. Livy writes that they knew a victory would mean following Hasdrubal to Italy, and did not want to leave Iberia.

The legions push into the gap. On the wings the cavalry skirmish without result. The Africans and the Poeni partly turn to strike the flanks of the Roman legions, but each also has an allied legion in front of it.
