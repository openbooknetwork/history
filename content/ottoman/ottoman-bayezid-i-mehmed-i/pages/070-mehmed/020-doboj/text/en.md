Hrvoje Vukčić Hrvatinić, Grand Duke of Bosnia and lord of the lands around [Jajce](@jajce-1415), loses Split and his Dalmatian islands after King Ostoja returns to the Bosnian throne with Hungarian backing. In 1414 he turns to Mehmed for help.

@image jajce-pliva-c585be

In June 1415 a Hungarian army under János Garai, János Maróti and Pál Csupor crosses the Sava and camps below the fortress of Doboj in the valley of the Bosna, with supplies brought up the river by boat. An Ottoman army under İshak Bey comes up from the south by Zenica and joins Hrvoje and other Bosnian lords. In the battle, fought on 6 August 1415, or about 10 August by another account, near Makljenovac south of Doboj, the Hungarian army is routed and its three commanders are taken prisoner.

@image doboj-bascinet-e13c56

Ottoman raiders then cross the Sava and plunder the Hungarian border lands, and raids into Bosnia follow year after year. Hrvoje dies in 1416 and is buried at Jajce. In the following years Ottoman raiders reach Croatia and Styria.
