The Ottoman wings attack first. On the crusader right the akıncıs come down from the plateau and the Rumelian sipahis ride against the banners on the slope. The Croats of [Franko Talovac](@varna-wings-talovac) and the bishops’ banners drive them back with bombards and hand guns and follow them in disorder, and more Ottoman horsemen fall on their flank.

The crusader right breaks and flees behind the army towards the marshes of the lake south of the town, making for the fortress of Galata across the bay, and most of its men are killed in the marshes. [Cesarini](@varna-wings-cesarini) flees with them. Only Talovac’s Croats get back behind the wagon fort.

On the crusader left the Anatolian sipahis attack, are thrown back and come on again. [John Hunyadi](@varna-wings-hunyadi) asks the king to wait until he returns and rides to the left with two companies of horsemen, and the Anatolians break and flee. Their beylerbey [Karaca Bey](@varna-wings-karaca) is killed. Kenneth Setton writes that [Murad](@varna-wings-murad) thinks of leaving the field when his wing gives way, and that the janissaries hold him back.
