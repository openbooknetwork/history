At dawn on 26 August 1922 the artillery opens fire on the Greek positions, and Mustafa Kemal watches from Kocatepe, a hill south west of [Afyon](@afyon). During the night the V Cavalry Corps has passed through a gap in the Greek line into the Greek rear. The First Army attacks the fortified heights from the south. By the evening of 27 August Afyon is taken and the Greek front is cut in two.
