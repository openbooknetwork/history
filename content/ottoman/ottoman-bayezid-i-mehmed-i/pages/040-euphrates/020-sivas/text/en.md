In the spring of 1398 Bayezid marches into Canik, the coast and hills around [Samsun](@samsun-1398), takes the town and ends the rule of the petty beys of the region. He is back in Bursa early in the summer. Of the Candar lands İsfendiyar keeps only [Sinop](@sinop).

That summer Kadı Burhaneddin of [Sivas](@sivas-1398) goes to war with Kara Yülük Osman of the Aq Qoyunlu, who surprises his camp and takes him prisoner. Kara Yülük besieges Sivas, and when Burhaneddin’s son refuses to surrender the town, he has Burhaneddin put to death, in July or August 1398. Burhaneddin’s son and emirs call on Bayezid. Schiltberger, who marches with the relief army, writes that Bayezid sends his eldest son with 40,000 men. Kara Yülük withdraws, and the lands from Kırşehir to Sivas pass to Bayezid with [Kayseri](@kayseri-1398) and [Tokat](@tokat-1398), which some accounts have him take already in 1393.

Barquq, the Mamluk sultan of Egypt and Syria, dies on 20 June 1399, and his son Faraj succeeds him at the age of 13. Bayezid holds that his agreements with Barquq have lapsed and marches against the Mamluk border. He takes [Malatya](@malatya-1399) from the Mamluks, and [Darende](@darende-1399), [Divriği](@divrigi-1399), [Behisni](@besni-1399) and Kâhta, castles held by the Dulkadir beys of Elbistan, vassals of the Mamluks. The Ottoman border reaches the Euphrates. Bayezid demands that Taharten, the emir of [Erzincan](@erzincan-1399), accept him as overlord, and Taharten turns to Timur.
