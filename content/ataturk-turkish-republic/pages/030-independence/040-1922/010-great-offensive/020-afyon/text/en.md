At dawn on 26 August 1922 Mustafa Kemal watches from Kocatepe, a hill south west of Afyon, as the artillery opens on the Greek positions. The First Army attacks the fortified heights from the south while the cavalry corps rides through a gap into the Greek rear. By the evening of 27 August Afyon is taken and the Greek front is cut in two.
