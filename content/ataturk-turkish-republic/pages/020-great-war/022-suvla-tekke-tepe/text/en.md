Two Ottoman divisions, the 7th and the 12th, march south from Bolayır and reach the Suvla front on the evening of 8 August. Their commander, Feyzi Bey, refuses to attack at once with tired men and no guns, and Liman von Sanders replaces him with Mustafa Kemal. Kemal now commands the whole Anafartalar sector, from Suvla south to Conkbayırı.

That night the British 32nd Brigade is ordered to march two and a half miles to the Tekke Tepe ridge. It nears the summit at about 4.00 on 9 August, shortly after the Ottoman reinforcements have reached it, and is met with a bayonet charge. Within minutes the brigade is broken and its survivors fall back towards the beach. With the high ground held at Suvla, Kemal turns to Conkbayırı and in the evening reconnoitres it for an attack.
