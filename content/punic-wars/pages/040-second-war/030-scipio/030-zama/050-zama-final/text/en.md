The Romans re-form into a single line, the hastati in the centre and the principes and triarii on the wings, to match the longer line of Hannibal’s veterans, now joined by men rallied from the first two lines. The two sides are about equal in number, and the veterans are fresh. Polybius writes that they charge each other with the greatest fire and fury, and the fight goes on for a long time without either side giving way.

Then Masinissa and Laelius return from the pursuit and charge the Carthaginian line from behind. The line collapses and a great massacre follows. Hannibal is one of the few who escape.
