On 24 August 1473 Mehmed marches to [Şebinkarahisar](@sebinkarahisar-surrender-1473). When his guns open fire on the walls, its lord Darab Bey surrenders the castle, and Mehmed leaves 1,000 men there with plenty of supplies. From Şebinkarahisar he sends letters that announce his victory, among them a decree of 30 August to the Turkomans of Anatolia, written in the Uyghur script.

In 1474 Mehmed dismisses the grand vizier Mahmud Pasha and has him executed, and Gedik Ahmed Pasha takes his place. Gedik Ahmed takes [Ermenek](@ermenek-1474) and the other castles that the Karaman princes still hold in the Taurus, and the lands around Niğde and Develi. Pir Ahmed gives up and goes to the Aq Qoyunlu, who give him a fief at [Bayburt](@bayburt-1474), where he dies in 1474. His brother Kasım holds [Silifke](@silifke-1475) and the coast for a while with Venetian help and loses his last lands in 1475.

Mehmed's son Mustafa falls ill after the campaign and dies at [Bor](@bor-1474) near Niğde on 25 December 1474. He is buried at Bursa, and his brother Cem becomes governor of Karaman at [Konya](@konya-1474). Uzun Hasan's son Uğurlu Mehmed rebels against his father in 1474, and in 1475 he flees to Mehmed, who makes him governor of Sivas and gives him his daughter Gevherhan in marriage.
