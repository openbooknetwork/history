Hasdrubal flees to [Carthage](@carthage-203), where he is removed from command. Laelius and Masinissa pursue Syphax to his capital, [Cirta](@cirta-203), defeat him again outside the city and capture him. Cirta surrenders to Masinissa, who takes over Syphax’s kingdom.

@image masinissa-coin-388057

Masinissa marries Sophonisba. Livy writes that when Scipio demands her surrender as the wife of an enemy king, Masinissa sends her poison and she drinks it. Scipio moves his army to [Tunis](@tunis-203), in sight of Carthage. Carthage opens peace talks and recalls Hannibal and Mago from Italy.
