Murad, the eldest son of Mehmed I, was born in June 1404 and grew up at [Amasya](@amasya-1421), which he has governed since his father became sole sultan in 1413. Mehmed dies at [Edirne](@edirne-1421b) on 26 May 1421, and the viziers keep his death secret until Murad has ridden from Amasya to [Bursa](@bursa-1421). There, in June 1421, he is proclaimed sultan at sixteen. His brother Mustafa, thirteen years old, governs Hamideli, and his younger brothers Yusuf and Mahmud are still children.

Murad inherits the lands his father reunited after the civil war. In Europe they reach from the Danube and Dobruja to Thessaly and Valona, and in Anatolia from İzmir to Ankara, Amasya, Samsun and Antalya. Constantinople, Thessalonica and the Morea are Byzantine, Wallachia pays tribute, and Stefan Lazarević rules the Serbian Despotate. In Anatolia the beys of Karaman, Germiyan, Menteşe, Teke and Candar rule their own lands.
