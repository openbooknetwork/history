Skanderbeg comes home in spring 1467 with a little money and grain from Ferrante of Naples. At [Lezhë](@lezhe-1468) (Alessio) he joins Lekë Dukagjini and the lords of the north, and he gathers 13,400 men by Demetrio Franco’s count, among them Venetian soldiers and men from [Durrës](@durres), Shkodër, Lezhë, Drisht and Bar. They march past the mouth of the Mat and through the woods of Jonima to the hills around Krujë, where Ballaban’s camp lies to the south-west of the town.

On 19 April Skanderbeg beats the Ottoman reinforcements under Ballaban’s brother Jonuz at [Krrabë](@krrabe-1467) and takes Jonuz and his son Haydar prisoner. Four days later the relief army and the garrison attack the besiegers together, and Ballaban is killed by a shot from the arquebusier Gjergj Aleksi. The 10,000 men left in the camp, by the count of the Neapolitan official Bernardino de Geraldinis, offer to give up the camp for a free passage, but Lekë Dukagjini and other lords refuse. The Ottomans cut their way out through Dibra, and Skanderbeg enters Krujë on 23 April 1467.

Skanderbeg then attacks [Elbasan](@elbasan-1466), but he has too few guns and men to take it. In summer 1467 Mehmed comes back by Berat, and Mahmud Pasha searches the mountains for fifteen days while Skanderbeg escapes to the coast. Mehmed besieges Krujë for a few days and withdraws, and his horsemen raid the lands around Shkodër and Durrës. In January 1468 Skanderbeg calls the Albanian lords to Lezhë, where he falls ill and dies on 17 January 1468, aged 62, and Venetian soldiers join the garrison of Krujë.
