At about 6 a.m., in thick fog, [Lannes’s](@jena-fog-lannes) corps advances from the Landgrafenberg with Suchet’s division on the right and Gazan’s on the left. They drive [Tauentzien’s](@jena-fog-tauentzien) advance guard out of Closewitz and Cospeda and back across the plateau towards Vierzehnheiligen. [Napoleon](@jena-fog-napoleon) keeps the Imperial Guard on the summit behind them.

The French flanks come up in turn. On the right [Saint-Hilaire’s](@jena-fog-soult) division of Soult’s IV Corps climbs from the Saale valley, attacks the Prussian left and cuts it off from the rest of [Hohenlohe’s](@jena-fog-hohenlohe) army. On the left [Augereau’s](@jena-fog-augereau) VII Corps marches up the Mühltal from Jena towards Isserstedt. Hohenlohe, who has misjudged the strength and direction of the attack, brings his divisions forward from their camps to form a line in front of Vierzehnheiligen, with the Saxons on his right.
