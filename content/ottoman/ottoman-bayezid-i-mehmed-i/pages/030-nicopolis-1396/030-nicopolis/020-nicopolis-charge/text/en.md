At daybreak on 25 September Sigismund asks the French to wait two hours until his scouts return. Coucy and Jean de Vienne advise waiting, but [the Count of Eu](@nicopolis-charge-eu) says Sigismund wants the honour of the battle for himself, and the French advance. Eu leads the vanguard, and [John of Nevers](@nicopolis-charge-nevers) and Coucy the main body. [Sigismund](@nicopolis-charge-sigismund) stays behind with the Hungarians, the Germans and the Knights Hospitaller, with the Transylvanians and Mircea’s Wallachians on his wings.

The French and their mounted archers ride south with their backs to the town, up the slope toward the Ottomans coming down from the hills. Their charge scatters the akıncı light horsemen in front of the Ottoman line and reaches the [azaps](@nicopolis-charge-azaps), foot soldiers and archers who stand behind rows of sharpened stakes. Arrows fall on the knights, horses are impaled on the stakes and many riders dismount to pull the stakes up, until the azaps break and flee behind the sipahis.
