On 1 September the order goes out: "Armies, your first objective is the Mediterranean. Forward." The cavalry covers four hundred kilometres in nine days through burning towns the retreating army leaves behind. Turkish troops enter İzmir on 9 September 1922. Four days later fire destroys the Greek and Armenian quarters of the city. By 18 September the last Greek units have left Anatolia.
