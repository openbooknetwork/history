Mehmed leads the next campaign against Moldavia himself and sets out in June 1476 with Basarab Laiotă's 12,000 Wallachians. At his order the Crimean Tatars break into Moldavia first, but Stephen defeats them and persuades the Tatars of the Great Horde to attack the Crimea, which draws the Crimean Tatars home. Near Varna Mehmed offers Stephen peace if he pays the tribute and gives up Kilia, and when Stephen refuses, the army crosses the Danube and enters Moldavia in late June.

Stephen lays waste to the land before the Ottomans and attacks their smaller detachments. On 26 July 1476 he makes a stand with 10,000 to 20,000 men in the White Valley near Războieni, with his back to a wooded hill and trenches and felled trees before him. Under the fire of his guns the janissaries lie down on the ground, until Mehmed rides forward with his shield and they follow him. By nightfall the Moldavians break, and Stephen escapes with a few men to the north-west of Moldavia or into Poland.

The Ottomans fail to take [Suceava](@suceava-1476) and [Neamț](@neamt-1476), hunger and disease break out in the camp, and an army under Stephen Báthory and Vlad III comes from Transylvania. In August Mehmed leaves Moldavia. In November Báthory and Vlad take Târgoviște and [Bucharest](@bucharest-1476), and Vlad becomes prince of Wallachia for the third time, but Basarab returns with Ottoman help, and Vlad is killed near [Snagov](@snagov-1477) in late December 1476 or early January 1477.
